# SanskaTots Meesho Ads Jargon Glossary
**Date:** 25 July 2026  
**Source:** Terms used across both Meesho 5X ROI reports

---

## A

### Ad-attributed orders
Orders that came through ad clicks (not purely organic discovery).

### Ad-attributed revenue / Attributed sales
Revenue credited to ads by the platform attribution window.

### AOV (Average Order Value)
Average revenue per order.  
Formula: `AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders`

---

## B

### Baseline
Your current starting performance level used for comparison (for example current CPC Rs 0.45).

### Bid
Maximum amount you are willing to pay per click in CPC advertising.

### Bid band
Recommended lower-upper bid range for a campaign/SKU.

### Bid ceiling
Maximum safe bid based on conversion and CPO target so profitability is protected.

### Blended ROAS
Overall ROAS across multiple campaigns/SKUs together, not just one campaign.

### Break-even
Point where profit is zero (no gain, no loss).

### Break-even CPO
Highest CPO you can tolerate before contribution becomes zero.  
Formula: `Break-even CPO = Contribution Before Ads`

### Break-even ROAS
Minimum ROAS required to not lose money.  
Formula: `Break-even ROAS = Settlement / Break-even CPO`

### Budget split
How total ad budget is divided across campaigns/ad types.

### Budget reallocation
Shifting budget from weak campaigns to strong campaigns based on data.

---

## C

### Campaign
A structured ad unit where you set budget, bidding, targeting, and product grouping.

### Campaign architecture
Overall design of campaigns (single-SKU, combo, intent clusters, etc.) for clear control.

### Cash break-even
Condition where ad + product economics do not generate loss, but also no profit.

### CMB campaign
Short label used for combo campaigns in the report (for example CMB1, CMB2).

### Combo campaign
Campaign promoting bundled products (2-book or 3-book sets), usually to improve AOV and margin.

### Contribution
Money left after key direct costs are removed.

### Contribution before ads
Settlement minus landed cost, before subtracting ad spend.  
Formula: `Contribution Before Ads = Settlement - Landed Cost`

### Contribution margin
Contribution expressed as an amount or percentage after direct costs.

### Contribution-level ROI
ROI measured after accounting for contribution economics, not just top-line sales.

### Conversion
When a click or visit becomes an order.

### Conversion rate / CVR
Percentage of clicks that become orders.  
Formula: `CVR = Orders / Clicks`

### CPO (Cost Per Order)
Average ad spend needed to generate one ad order.  
Formula: `CPO = Ad Spend / Ad Orders`

### CPC (Cost Per Click)
Average amount paid for one ad click.

### CPC-led strategy
Operating model where manual CPC campaigns are primary control lever.

### CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Percentage of impressions that became clicks.  
Formula: `CTR = Clicks / Impressions`

---

## D

### Data maturity
How reliable your campaign data is based on order volume and consistency over time.

### Discovery
Early stage where ads are used to find what audience, listing, and offer converts best.

---

## E

### Execution dashboard
Operational metric sheet used weekly/daily for decisions (ROAS, CPO, CTR, CVR, etc.).

---

## G

### Green zone
Healthy performance range where scaling is allowed (in reports: ROAS >= 5x).

### Growth milestone
Time-bound target checkpoint (for example day 14, day 30, day 60 order targets).

---

## H

### Hard stop CPO
Non-negotiable upper CPO limit. If crossed, campaign should be paused or repaired.

### Hero campaign / Hero SKU
Top-priority SKU/campaign expected to drive high quality volume and stable returns.

### Hybrid strategy
Using both CPC and Smart ROI together instead of only one ad mode.

---

## I

### Impressions
Number of times your ad/listing was shown.

### Intent cluster campaign
Campaign grouping related products by similar buyer intent (for example culture books cluster).

---

## K

### KPI (Key Performance Indicator)
Critical metric used to judge performance (CPO, ROAS, CVR, CTR, etc.).

---

## L

### Landed cost
Total per-unit product cost to get item ready for sale (product + inward costs as defined by you).

### Learning stage / Learning phase
Initial campaign period where data is collected and frequent optimization is required.

### Listing conversion quality
How well product listing content converts traffic into orders.

---

## M

### Margin
Difference between revenue and costs; indicates profit room.

### Mature winners
Campaigns/SKUs with enough stable data and strong performance to scale.

### Max CPO
Maximum allowed CPO as per profitability/ROAS target.

### Meesho settlement
Net amount received from Meesho per order after platform-side deductions included in settlement.

---

## O

### Objective (campaign objective)
Primary purpose of a campaign, such as traffic, conversion, AOV lift, or profitability.

### Operating band
Defined performance range for decisions (green/yellow/red zones).

### Organic orders
Orders that happen without ad attribution, usually from search/discovery/repeat behavior.

### Optimization loop
Repeated cadence (for example every 72 hours) to adjust bids, budgets, and creatives based on data.

---

## P

### Pause/repair
Action framework: stop spending on underperforming campaign, then fix listing/offer/bid and relaunch.

### Per-book campaign
Campaign with one SKU only, used for signal clarity.

### Premium SKU
Higher-price SKU generally expected to deliver higher basket value.

---

## R

### Red zone
Poor performance range requiring correction or pause (in reports: ROAS below 4x).

### Review velocity
Speed at which new customer reviews are generated over time.

### ROI (Return on Investment)
Profit generated relative to investment.  
Generic formula: `ROI = (Net Return / Investment) x 100`

### ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
Revenue generated for each rupee of ad spend.  
Formula: `ROAS = Attributed Sales / Ad Spend`

### RTO (Return to Origin)
Order returned back before successful delivery (commonly COD-related). A major margin risk.

---

## S

### Scale / Scaling
Increasing budget/spend in controlled steps once performance is proven.

### Seasonal campaign
Campaign aligned to festivals/events/seasonal demand windows.

### Settlement value
Per-order credited amount used as revenue basis in these reports.

### Signal clarity
Decision quality from clean data; improved when campaigns are not over-grouped.

### Single-SKU campaign
Campaign where only one product is advertised, allowing cleaner control and diagnosis.

### Smart ROI ads
Automated ad mode optimized by platform toward return metrics, usually better after conversion signals accumulate.

### Social proof
Trust signals like ratings, reviews, and review comments.

### SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)
Documented repeatable process to run operations consistently.

### Stage-wise plan (Phase 1/2/3)
Time-based execution plan where budget and strategy evolve by data maturity.

### Threshold
A predefined metric limit used to trigger actions (scale, hold, pause).

### Traffic SKU
SKU used to generate clicks and reach, sometimes with lower direct profitability.

---

## T

### Target CPO
Desired CPO level to maintain the chosen ROAS/profit objective.

### Tier A / Tier B / Tier C campaigns
Priority buckets used in campaign architecture:
- Tier A: Hero single-SKU campaigns
- Tier B: Intent-cluster campaigns
- Tier C: Combo-focused campaigns

---

## U

### Unit economics
Per-unit financial model (settlement, landed cost, contribution, ad limits).

---

## Y

### Yellow zone
Caution range where campaign is not bad enough to kill, but not strong enough to scale aggressively.

---

## Quick Formula Sheet
- `CTR = Clicks / Impressions`
- `CVR = Orders / Clicks`
- `CPO = Ad Spend / Ad Orders`
- `ROAS = Attributed Sales / Ad Spend`
- `Contribution Before Ads = Settlement - Landed Cost`
- `Max CPO for 5x ROAS = Settlement / 5`
- `Break-even ROAS = Settlement / Contribution Before Ads`

---

Use this glossary as a decision dictionary. If any team member uses a metric without formula context, ask for the exact calculation and date range before taking budget decisions.
