## 2. WHO HE ACTUALLY IS — THE BACKSTORY

Rajiv started this 18–25 years ago. He probably:

- Watched the **Kidzee / EuroKids / Bachpan / Tree House** explosion of the 2005–2015 era and either rode that wave as a master franchisee or built his own competing brand.
- Took his **first 10 centers personally** — knows every original franchisee by first name, attended their daughter's wedding, lent some of them money during COVID.
- Survived **2020–2022 COVID disaster** when preschool footfall collapsed to zero for 18 months. He lost 40–60 centers permanently. This trauma still shapes every decision.
- Watched **BYJU's, WhiteHat Jr, and ed-tech collapse** between 2023–2025 and now deeply distrusts anyone who pitches "tech-enabled learning."
- Saw **NEP 2020** as either the biggest threat (must restructure entire curriculum) or biggest opportunity (foundational stage 3–8 years is now officially recognized).
- Personally hates that **parents perceive his centers as "glorified daycare"** — he genuinely believes in early childhood education but the business model forces compromise.

**He is NOT a fresh entrepreneur. He is a tired, scarred, suspicious, deeply-networked operator.**

## 5. TOP 10 DAILY FRUSTRATIONS

Ranked by what actually makes him swear under his breath:

1. **"Why does every franchisee think their problem is the only problem?"** — 200 centers means 200 unique fires. Each one feels like an emergency to the franchisee.
2. **"Parents trust the teacher more than my brand."** — Brand equity dies when a popular teacher leaves a center and parents follow her to a competitor.
3. **"Curriculum vendors over-promise and under-deliver."** — He's been burned 4–5 times by suppliers who showed glossy samples then shipped poor-quality material at scale.
4. **"My academic team designs for ideal classrooms. Reality is a Tier-3 teacher with 22 toddlers and one assistant."** — Constant gap between curriculum design and ground execution.
5. **"Every parent is now a YouTube expert."** — Parents quote Montessori, Reggio Emilia, Charlotte Mason at PTA meetings and challenge his curriculum.
6. **"My franchisees secretly buy from Amazon and cut me out of the supply margin."** — Curriculum revenue leakage is real and growing.
7. **"Teacher attrition is destroying consistency."** — 35–50% annual teacher turnover means parents see a new face every term.
8. **"Compliance is endless."** — NEP 2020, state-level RTE rules, fire safety, food licenses, vaccination protocols — the paperwork never stops.
9. **"Social media can kill us overnight."** — One angry parent video can wipe out 5 years of brand-building in 48 hours.
10. **"My investors want growth. My franchisees want stability. My parents want innovation. All three are mutually exclusive."**

### The deeper, almost-existential frustration:
> *"I'm building a brand that touches 80,000 children every day, and I still don't know if I'm actually making a difference in their lives — or just running an expensive daycare with a curriculum sticker on it."*

## 8. DECISION-MAKING STYLE — HOW HE BUYS

### He does NOT decide alone.
He has a **buying committee** even if he never calls it that:

| Role | Person | What They Care About |
|---|---|---|
| **Final approval** | Rajiv (Founder/MD) | Brand fit, ROI, strategic positioning, risk |
| **Academic veto** | Head of Curriculum / Academic Director (often a 50-something woman with M.Ed.) | Pedagogical soundness, age-appropriateness, NEP alignment |
| **Operations check** | COO / Supply Chain Head | Can we deliver this to 400 centers reliably? At what unit economics? |
| **Franchisee voice** | Regional Heads (4–8 of them) | Will my franchisees actually push this? Will parents pay for it? |
| **Finance gate** | CFO | Working capital, margin, exclusivity terms, payment cycle |
| **Influencer (silent)** | His spouse / co-founder | She visits centers. She trusts her gut. She vetoes things he likes. |

> **Insight:** *Sell to Rajiv, but you must arm the Academic Director with talking points and the COO with logistics confidence — or the deal dies in committee 3 weeks after the pitch.*

### His buying psychology
- **Trust takes 6–12 months to build** — first meeting to first PO is rarely under 90 days.
- He needs to **see it in a real classroom** before he commits at scale.
- He runs **2–3 center pilots** before any network rollout.
- He **calls 2–3 peer founders** to ask "have you heard of these guys?" before any major decision.
- Once convinced, he becomes **the most loyal customer in B2B** — multi-year contracts, exclusive co-branding deals, willing to give a 5-year roadmap commitment.

## 12. WHAT WOULD FINALLY CONVINCE HIM TO BUY

There is no "single trigger." It's a **stack of 5–7 signals** he needs to see together. Miss one and he walks.

### The Conviction Stack

#### Signal 1 — Pedagogical credibility (table stakes)
- A **3–5 page academic dossier** showing alignment to NEP 2020 Foundational Stage outcomes, Montessori principles, and developmental milestones for ages 1–5.
- One **respected academic advisor** on SanskaTots' board (a recognized early childhood educator, NCERT/SCERT alum, or M.Ed/Ph.D. in ECCE).
- A **white paper** showing learning outcomes: "kids using Velcro-based reusable activity books showed X% improvement in pencil grip / pre-writing skills over Y weeks."

#### Signal 2 — Manufacturing & supply confidence
- An **invitation to visit the printing/manufacturing facility** in Bangalore (this single move closes 30% of B2B deals).
- A **capacity statement**: "We can deliver 100,000 units in 90 days with 99.5% quality consistency."
- **ISO / BIS / non-toxic certifications** — physically shown, not just claimed.
- A **named supply chain head** he can call directly if anything goes wrong.

#### Signal 3 — Pilot proof
- A **free pilot in 3–5 of his centers** for 60 days, with structured before/after parent feedback collection.
- The pilot must include **a teacher training session** (online or on-site) so he sees you understand classroom reality.
- A **printed parent feedback report** at the end of the pilot — not a digital dashboard, a printed report he can show his academic committee.

#### Signal 4 — Co-branding & exclusivity
- Willingness to **co-brand the books** with his chain logo on cover + welcome letter from him in every kit.
- **Category exclusivity** in the preschool franchise channel for 2–3 years — *"You won't sell to Kidzee or EuroKids."*
- **Custom SKU development** — books designed specifically for his curriculum sequence.

#### Signal 5 — Commercial structure that respects his model
- **45–60 day credit period** (not upfront payment).
- **Volume pricing in tiers**: 5K / 25K / 50K / 100K units annually.
- **Margin transparency** — show him what franchisees pay vs what HQ pays, so he can build his own margin stack.
- A **5-year price-lock** so he can plan with confidence.

#### Signal 6 — Peer signal
- A **case study from one existing chain** (even a 20-center boutique chain counts) saying "we adopted SanskaTots and parent NPS moved from X to Y."
- A **named reference founder** he can WhatsApp directly without your team in the loop.
- Mention from a **respected industry voice** (EducationWorld, FICCI ECCE wing, ScooNews) — even a small feature counts.

#### Signal 7 — Founder-to-founder trust
- **Prashanth (SanskaTots founder) must meet Rajiv in person** — at least once. Not a sales rep. Not a BD head. Founder-to-founder.
- A **shared philosophical conviction** — Rajiv needs to feel SanskaTots' founder believes in screen-free, hands-on, culturally-rooted learning as a *mission*, not as a *marketing line*.
- A **handwritten note** (or genuinely personal LinkedIn DM) after the first meeting. Old-school. Works on 50-year-old founders almost without exception.

## 13. THE OPENING LINE / HOOK THAT WORKS

Avoid:
- ❌ "We're a Montessori brand on Amazon..."
- ❌ "We have great products at competitive prices..."
- ❌ "Would you like to see our catalogue?"

Use:
- ✅ *"Rajiv-ji, I'm not here to pitch you books. I want to ask you one question: if you could give every one of your 60,000 students a take-home kit that their mother takes a picture of and posts on Instagram tagging your brand — what would you pay for that per child per year?"*

Or:
- ✅ *"Your biggest competitor's curriculum looks identical to yours when a mom does an admission walkthrough. We can change that in 90 days, with one co-branded SKU line."*

Or (the NEP angle):
- ✅ *"NEP 2020 foundational stage compliance audits start in 2027. We've already mapped our entire catalogue to every outcome NCERT published. Want to see it before your academic team has to scramble?"*
