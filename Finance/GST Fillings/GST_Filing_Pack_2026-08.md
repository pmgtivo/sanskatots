## Basis of classification

| HSN | Description | Treatment | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4901 | Printed books, including Braille books | Exempt | Notfn. 2/2017-CT(R) Sch. Sl. No. 119 |
| 4902 | Newspapers, journals and periodicals, whether or not illustrated or containing advertising material | Exempt | Notfn. 2/2017-CT(R) Sch. Sl. No. 120 |
| 4903 | Children's picture, drawing or colouring books | Exempt | Notfn. 2/2017-CT(R) Sch. Sl. No. 121 |
| 4905 | Maps and hydrographic or similar charts of all kinds, including atlases, wall maps, topographical plans and globes, printed | Exempt | Notfn. 2/2017-CT(R) Sch. Sl. No. 123 |

- **Exempt, not nil-rated.** Printed books are exempted under section 11 via Notfn. 2/2017-CT(R). They are absent from the rate notification 1/2017, which is what 'nil rated' means. The GSTR-1 Table 8 column differs; the GSTR-3B box (3.1(c)) is the same.
- **No ITC.** Section 17(2) blocks credit attributable to exempt supplies, so the 18% GST on marketplace commission is a cost.
- **Bill of supply, not tax invoice.** Section 31(3)(c) read with rule 49 applies to exempt supplies.
- **No TCS on exempt supplies.** Section 52(1) charges TCS on the net value of *taxable* supplies only.
- **Registration still mandatory.** Section 24(ix) requires registration for persons supplying goods through an e-commerce operator.
