## 6. THE 30% DAMAGE ASSUMPTION — WHAT IT COSTS YOU

- Units that came back this month (Return + RTO): **68 units**, carrying **₹12,607** of manufacturing cost value.
- **Your instruction this month: 30% of returned stock is damaged and unsellable.** So:
  - **₹3,782 is written off as a straight loss** (damaged, no salvage value).
  - **₹8,825 worth of stock is assumed to rejoin sellable inventory** (undamaged, can be re-shipped).
- **This is a looser assumption than August's 50/50 split.** Applying August's 50% damage rate to this same September batch would push the write-off to **~₹6,304** — a **₹2,522 difference**, purely from the assumption, not from more/fewer returns happening.
- **Recommendation carried over from August, still unresolved:** you haven't yet told me the *actual* observed resale rate. Track returned units as "resold" vs "scrapped" for 2–3 months. Until then, treat net profit as sitting somewhere between ₹31,039 (30% damage) and ~₹28,500 (50% damage) depending on which assumption proves true.

## 10. SEPTEMBER vs AUGUST — WHAT CHANGED

1. **Net profit up 28% (₹24,231→₹31,039)** — driven by a richer sales mix (higher-ASP SKUs like ST-ABB-02/03 and the new ₹450-cost Gift Pack), not by volume (units sold actually fell 5.8%).

3. **ROAS fell from 8.55x to 6.22x** — ad spend grew faster (+51%) than gross sale (+10%). Still healthy, but the trend needs watching next month.
4. **The 30% vs 50% write-off policy change flatters this month's profit by roughly ₹2,500** relative to August's assumption — factor this in before comparing month-on-month growth to your team/investors.
5. **Montessori Busy Book (ST-MBB-01) is now a two-month-running problem** — return rate went from 13.04% (Aug) to 12.64% (Sep, roughly flat) but RTO jumped from 0.62% to 6.59%. This SKU alone needs a dedicated fix, not just a "watch."

## 11. METHOD NOTES

- **Basis:** order date, not payment date. Orders placed 01–30 Sep 2026 only; rows placed in August excluded even though they appear in the same source files.
- **"Books sold" = 484** = 414 delivered + 69 in transit + 1 exchange. In-transit orders are booked at expected settlement.
- **Return/RTO % use closed orders as the denominator** (552 − 69 in transit = 483).
- **Damage write-off = 30% of returned/RTO stock treated as unsellable this month**, per your explicit instruction — a judgment call, not observed data, and a change from August's 50% assumption. This materially affects month-on-month comparability (see Section 10, point 4) — recommend fixing one resale rate going forward once you have real return-inspection data.
- **Ads allocated pro-rata by gross sale**, since Meesho's ad file reports at campaign level, not SKU level — same limitation as August.
- One suborder (`321324974769342529_1` type pattern, e.g. also seen on `SiGenhwc`) shows a real status transition (Shipped→Return) without an explicit FEE_ADJ tag, meaning it's counted as 2 "orders" in the row-count sense — a handful of cases (~2 suborders company-wide this month), consistent with how August was measured, not a new bug.
- Ad spend attributed by date ads **ran**, not date deducted; ₹1,942 of August-run ads excluded from this report.
- This month includes both a "Previous Payment" (settled) and an "Outstanding Payment" (unsettled) file — **~50% of September's earned settlement is still receivable in October**, unlike August where both files were fully settled.
- **Excludes** salaries, rent, utilities, design, software, and all non-Meesho channels. This is a **channel contribution statement**, not a company P&L.
- Manufacturing costs for `ST-IMB-01` (₹160), `ST-SM-CB` (₹450), and `SiGenhwc` (₹58) were confirmed directly by you for this report — not previously on file.
- Reproduce: `Finance/Sep_Profit_Loss/september_book_pnl.py` (book level) · `september_cohort_pnl.py` (SKU level + sensitivity, ACOS/TACOS/ROAS).
