## 6. THE 50% DAMAGE ASSUMPTION — WHAT IT COSTS YOU

- Units that came back this month (Return + RTO): **56 units**, carrying **₹9,961** of manufacturing cost.
- **Your instruction: 50% of returned stock is damaged and unsellable.** So:
  - **₹4,981 is written off as a straight loss** (damaged, no salvage value).
  - **₹4,980 worth of stock is assumed to rejoin sellable inventory** (undamaged, can be re-shipped).
- This is **materially more conservative** than a 70%-resellable assumption — on this same batch of returned stock, 70% resellable would put the write-off at ~₹2,988 instead of ₹4,981, a **₹1,990 additional hit to net profit** purely from the policy change, not from more returns happening.
- **Recommendation:** Track actual resale rate of returned stock for 2–3 months (tag returned units as "resold" vs "scrapped" when they come back to your warehouse). If real resale is closer to 70%, you're under-stating profit by ~₹3,000/month with the current 50% assumption; if it's genuinely 50% or worse, this report is your true number and you should investigate *why* damage rates are this high (packaging quality, courier handling, or product fragility).

## 13. METHOD NOTES

- **Basis:** order date, not payment date. Orders placed 01–31 Aug 2026 only; rows placed in July or September excluded even though they appear in the same source files.
- **"Books sold" = 514** = 510 delivered + 4 in transit. In-transit orders are booked at expected settlement.
- **Return/RTO % use closed orders as the denominator** (569 − 4 in transit = 565). Using all orders would understate the rate.
- **Damage write-off changed this run: 50% of returned/RTO stock is treated as unsellable** (previously 70% was assumed resellable). This is a judgment call from you, not observed data — track actual resale outcomes to validate it (see Section 6).
- **Ads allocated pro-rata by gross sale**, since Meesho's ad file reports at campaign level, not SKU level. A campaign-to-SKU mapping would sharpen per-book ACOS meaningfully.
- 54 fee-adjustment rows (affiliate fee, claims) were attributed to their book/SKU but not counted as orders.
- Ad spend attributed by date ads **ran**, not date deducted. ₹1,548 of July-run ads excluded from this report.
