## 2. PRODUCT IDEAS — High Sales Potential

### A. BUNDLES (Zero New Design Cost — Use Existing Books)
Bundles are the **#1 sales multiplier** on Amazon India. Indian parents over-index on "value combos." Bundles also increase AOV (avg order value) from ₹399 → ₹1,200+.

#### A1. "Pre-Preschool Starter Kit" (Hero Bundle)
- **Contents:** Pencil Control L1 + Mini Alphabet Tracing + Colors & Shapes + Number 1-20 + Rhymes
- **Price:** ₹999 (vs ₹1,495 individual)
- **Positioning:** "Everything your 2-year-old needs before preschool"
- **Volume target:** 800–1,200 units/month
- **Why it sells:** One-click decision, gift-ready, beats competitors who sell ₹299 single books

#### A2. "Busy Book Trio" (Premium Velcro Bundle)
- **Contents:** Animals Busy Book + Body Parts Busy Book + Get To Know Around
- **Price:** ₹1,299 (vs ₹1,797)
- **Positioning:** "30 days of screen-free learning — Velcro magic"
- **Why it sells:** Highest profit margin category; premium gifting

#### A3. "My First Indian Roots Bundle"
- **Contents:** India Board Book + Shloka Board Book + BhagavadGita for Kids + Rhymes
- **Price:** ₹1,099
- **Positioning:** "Indian values from age 2 — gift for grandparents to give"
- **Why it sells:** Grandparent gifting market (huge, untapped), NRI parents in US/UK/Singapore

#### A4. "Kannada Home Learning Bundle" (Regional Power Play)
- **Contents:** Kannada Alphabets Tracing + Learn Kannada 5-in-1 + Shloka Book
- **Price:** ₹899
- **Positioning:** "Karnataka moms — start Kannada at home"
- **Why it sells:** ZERO competition in regional bundles; replicate for Hindi, Tamil, Telugu

#### A5. "Daily 15-Min Routine Box" (Subscription-Style One-Time)
- **Contents:** 30 worksheets pulled from 5 books + activity calendar + parent guide
- **Price:** ₹599
- **Positioning:** "30 days of guided learning — open the calendar, do today's page"
- **Why it sells:** Solves the "what do I do today, mom?" problem; viral Instagram potential
