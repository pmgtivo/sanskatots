# PART 1: THE DIGITAL MARKETING LANDSCAPE 2026

## 1.1 Key Digital Trends Impacting SanskaTots

### **Trend 1: Discovery is Interest-Led, Not Follower-Led**
Social platforms now use micro-behaviors (hover time, rewatches, pauses) to surface content. This means **a new brand with zero followers can still go viral** if content resonates with audience interests. SanskaTots' cultural content (mythology, shlokas) is uniquely positioned for algorithm-driven discovery.

### **Trend 2: Social Media = Search Engine for Gen Z & Millennials**
> **63% of U.S. consumers use social media for search** (eMarketer 2025)
> **Over 50% of Gen Z prefers TikTok/Instagram over Google for product discovery**

**Implication:** SanskaTots must treat Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts as search optimization channels, not just brand awareness tools. Use keyword-rich captions like "activity book for 2 year old" and "Kannada tracing book."

### **Trend 3: Short-Form Video Dominates (43% Top-Performing Format)**
Short-form video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) is the **#1 performing content type** across all platforms. SanskaTots' founder story, product unboxings, and cultural content are perfect for this format.

### **Trend 4: Human-Made Authenticity Wins**
> **30% of consumers are LESS likely to buy from brands using AI-generated ads** (eMarketer)

This is a massive advantage for SanskaTots: founder-led storytelling, behind-the-scenes manufacturing content, and cultural authenticity cannot be replicated by larger competitors using AI.

### **Trend 5: Creator Partnerships Shift from Reach to ROI**
Follower count is becoming irrelevant. Brands now prioritize **trust, audience alignment, and storytelling quality**. Micro-influencers (5K-50K followers) with engaged mom communities outperform celebrity endorsements.

### **Trend 6: Third-Party Cookies Phase-Out (2025)**
Google has phased out third-party cookies. This means:
- **First-party data collection is critical** (email lists, WhatsApp communities)
- **Contextual advertising** and **organic social** become more valuable
- **Retargeting via platforms** (Amazon DSP, Meta pixel) still works

Amazon is not optional for SanskaTots. It's the **primary revenue channel** for Year 1. Here's how to dominate:

### 2.1.1 Amazon SEO (A9 Algorithm Optimization)

**The A9 algorithm ranks products based on:**
1. **Relevance** (keywords in title, bullets, backend)
2. **Conversion rate** (how many visitors buy)
3. **Sales velocity** (recent sales volume)
4. **Reviews & ratings**

**AGGRESSIVE KEYWORD STRATEGY:**

| Priority | Primary Keywords (High Volume) | Long-Tail Keywords |
|----------|-------------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | activity book for kids | velcro activity book for 2 year old toddler |

| 3 | kannada learning book | kannada alphabet tracing book for kids |
| 4 | bhagavad gita for kids | indian mythology story book for children |
| 5 | pencil control book | pre writing tracing book for preschool |

**Title Formula (Max 200 Characters):**
```
[Brand] + [Primary Keyword] + [Key Feature] + [Age Range] + [Material/Pages] + [Secondary Keyword]

Example:
SanskaTots All-in-One Activity Busy Book for Toddlers 1-4 Years | 20+ Velcro Activities | Montessori Learning Toy | Fine Motor Skills Development
```

**Backend Search Terms (Fill ALL 250 bytes):**

- Competitor brand names (yes, allowed): "like little minds at work"
- Occasion keywords: "birthday gift for 2 year old girl"

### 2.1.2 Amazon PPC Strategy

**Phase 1 (Month 1-2): Keyword Discovery**
- Budget: ₹300-500/day
- Campaign Type: Auto campaigns only
- Goal: Let Amazon discover which keywords convert

**Phase 2 (Month 3-4): Scale Winners**
- Budget: ₹800-1,500/day
- Campaign Types: Manual Exact Match (winners from auto) + Brand Defense
- Target ACoS: 30-35% (acceptable for new products)

**Phase 3 (Month 5+): Defend & Expand**
- Budget: ₹2,000-3,000/day
- Add Sponsored Brands (video + headline)
- Product Targeting (competitor ASINs)
- Target ACoS: 20-25%

**PPC Rules:**
| Metric | Action |
|--------|--------|
| ACoS > 50% | Pause keyword |
| CTR < 0.3% | Negative exact match |
| Conversion > 10% | Increase bid 20% |
| Impressions < 100/week | Increase bid or broaden match |

### 2.1.3 Review Velocity Strategy

**Reviews are EVERYTHING on Amazon. Target: 50+ reviews on top ASINs by Month 3.**

**Tactics:**

2. **Amazon Request a Review Button**
   - Use Seller Central to request review 5-15 days post-delivery

4. **Amazon Vine Program** (When eligible)
