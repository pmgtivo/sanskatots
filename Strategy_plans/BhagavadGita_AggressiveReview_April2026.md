## CRITICAL ISSUES (Must Fix)

### 1. DUPLICATE VERSES — 3 instances of the same shloka used twice

| Verse | Used on Page | Used AGAIN on Page | Problem |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|---------|
| **BG 6.5** | Page 5 (Friend or Enemy) | Page 16 (Never Give Up) | EXACT same verse, almost same lesson |
| **BG 6.35** | Page 2 (Practice Makes Perfect) | Page 24 (Focus Your Mind) | EXACT same verse, overlapping message |
| **BG 2.14** | Page 13 (Everything Changes) | Page 15 (Patience) | EXACT same verse, similar theme |

**Impact:** A parent reading this book will immediately notice the repetition. It looks lazy and reduces credibility. Each page MUST have a unique verse.

**Fix suggestions:**
- Page 16 (Never Give Up): Use BG 18.78 ("Where there is Krishna, there is victory") or BG 2.3 ("Do not yield to weakness, Arjuna")
- Page 24 (Focus Your Mind): Use BG 6.26 ("Wherever the restless mind wanders, one must bring it back under the control of the Self")
- Page 15 (Patience): Use BG 2.15 ("The person who is not disturbed by these and remains steady in pain and pleasure becomes fit for immortality")

---

### 2. WRONG SANSKRIT TERM — Page 5

Page 5 uses **"ATMA-SHAKTI"** (which means "soul power / self-strength") but the lesson is about **being your own friend or enemy**. The verse BG 6.5 uses the concept of Atma as Bandhu (friend) or Ripu (enemy).

**Correct term should be:** "Atma-Bandhu" or simply "Atman" — NOT "Atma-Shakti"

---

### 3. SPELLING ERROR — Page 18

**"Krutagnata"** → Should be **"Kritajnata"** (कृतज्ञता). The 'j' sound is missing entirely. Even in common Hindi transliteration, it's "Krutagnyata" or "Kritajnata" — never "Krutagnata."

---

### 4. AGE RANGE MISMATCH — Fundamental Design Flaw

The book claims **age 3–10 years**, but:
- 3-5 year olds CANNOT read or understand sentences like: *"Your happiness shouldn't depend on what the world does — it should come from inside you"*
- Concepts like impermanence, sense control, and Swadharma are abstract even for 8-year-olds
- Cricket references (Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, MS Dhoni) mean nothing to a 3-year-old

**Fix:** Either change the age range to **5–10 years** (or even **6–10**), OR create two separate versions (picture-based for 3-5, story-based for 6-10).

## DESIGN/LAYOUT ISSUES

### 5. A5 Page is TOO SMALL for the described layout

On a single A5 page, you're trying to fit:
- Page number circle
- Title + Sanskrit term
- "Krishna Says" quote box
- Explanation (4-5 sentences)
- 4-panel comic strip
- Large main illustration with speech bubble

**This is physically impossible on A5 without unreadably tiny text.** Either:
- Each "page" must be a **2-page SPREAD** (left = text + comic, right = illustration)
- Or upgrade to **A4 format**
- Or reduce content per page (remove either the comic OR the explanation)

### 6. Comic Strip Format Description is INCONSISTENT

- Pages 1-14 header: "bad choice → consequence → **realisation** → good choice"
- Pages 15-28 header: "bad choice → consequence → **good choice** → positive outcome"
- Design instructions: "Problem → Consequence → Realisation → Solution"

Pick ONE flow and stick to it across both files.

## TYPOGRAPHY & FORMATTING CONSISTENCY

### Issues Found:

1. **Quotation mark inconsistency** — Some pages use straight quotes " ", others use curly/smart quotes " ". Standardize throughout.

2. **Shloka citation formatting** — Some have *(Bhagavad Gita 2.47)* with italics, others vary. Keep format consistent: ***(Bhagavad Gita X.Y)***

3. **Section headers** — Mix of bold + ALL CAPS, some with only bold. Standardize to **HEADER NAME (Sanskrit Term)** format.

4. **Explanation text** — Varies between 4-5 sentences — some are much longer. Trim to exactly 4-5 sentences max per page.

5. **Comic strip descriptions** — Some have detailed narrative, others just list panels. Choose ONE style and apply consistently.
