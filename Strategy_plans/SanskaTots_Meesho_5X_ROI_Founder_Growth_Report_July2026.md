## 2) The Core Decision: CPC Ads or Smart ROI Ads?

## Final answer
Run **hybrid**, not either/or.
- **Phase 1 (next 14 days):** 70% budget in CPC, 30% in Smart ROI
- **Phase 2 (day 15 to day 45):** 50% CPC, 50% Smart ROI (only proven SKUs)
- **Phase 3 (day 46+):** 30% CPC, 70% Smart ROI for stable scale

### Why this is correct
- CPC gives manual control for new catalog learning and keyword-market fit.
- Smart ROI is better for scale once enough conversion signals exist.
- You currently have low review depth, so full Smart ROI immediately may under-deliver.
- Full CPC forever will create micromanagement load and ceiling on scale.

## 3) 5X ROI Definition You Must Use
Do not optimise only on ROAS. Optimise on contribution-level ROI.

Use this per SKU:

`Ad ROI = (Attributed Revenue - Product Cost - Platform + logistics costs) / Ad Spend`

Also track:

`CPO = Ad Spend / Ad Orders`

`ROAS = Attributed Revenue / Ad Spend`

### Practical guardrails
- Scale only if campaign has at least 20 ad orders and CPO within threshold for 7 days.
- Pause if CPO breaches threshold for 3 straight days.
- Never scale spend because impressions increased; scale only when contribution per order is positive.

## 4) Campaign Architecture (Exact Build)

## Rule for grouping books
Do not run one campaign with all books together from day 1.
Use this 3-tier structure:
- **Tier A Hero Campaigns:** 1 SKU per campaign (highest margin + strongest conversion)
- **Tier B Intent Cluster Campaigns:** 2 to 3 related books per campaign
- **Tier C Combo Campaigns:** combo packs only

### How many books per campaign?
- New/weak data SKU: 1 SKU per campaign
- Medium data SKU: max 2 SKUs in one campaign
- Mature winners with similar AOV/conversion: max 3 SKUs
- Never mix low-ticket traffic SKU and premium SKU in same campaign during learning stage

## 6) Bid Strategy (Using Your Rs 0.45 CPC Baseline)

Your current CPC is good. Preserve efficiency while scaling.

### CPC bid bands
- Hero SKUs: Rs 0.45 to Rs 0.70
- Traffic SKU: Rs 0.35 to Rs 0.55
- Combo packs: Rs 0.50 to Rs 0.80 (higher allowed if conversion holds)

### Bid optimisation loop
- Every 72 hours, review CTR, CVR, CPO, ROI
- If CTR high but CVR low: fix listing, do not raise bid
- If CTR low and CVR high: raise bid 10 to 15%
- If CPO rises >20% from baseline for 3 days: cut bid 10 to 20%

Best practice for your stage:
- Run per-book campaigns for discovery and control
- Run combo campaigns for profitability and ROI expansion

### Recommended mix
- 60% spend on single-book heroes
- 25% spend on combos
- 15% spend on clusters/new tests

- 40% combos

Reason:
