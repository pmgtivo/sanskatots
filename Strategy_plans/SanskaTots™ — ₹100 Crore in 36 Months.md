3. **Offline retail is the ₹50cr unlock** — E-commerce alone caps at ₹30–40cr. Retail distribution is where Skillmatics, Einstein Box, and every ₹100cr kid brand makes the bulk of revenue.

4. **Don't run out of cash** — Inventory-heavy business. Raise money BEFORE you need it. Keep 3 months of runway minimum.

5. **Diwali is your Super Bowl** — October-November should be 3x–4x your average month. Plan inventory 90 days in advance.

6. **Community before scale** — 1,000 true fan parents who recommend you to other parents > 100,000 one-time buyers.
