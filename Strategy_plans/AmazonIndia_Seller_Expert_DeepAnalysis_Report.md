## SECTION 1 — ACCOUNT & REGISTRATION MISTAKES (Pre-Launch)

### 1.1 Choosing the Wrong Seller Account Type
- **Mistake:** Registering as "Individual" seller to "save ₹999/month."
  - Individual = ₹0 fixed but ₹4 closing fee per item + cannot use FBA properly + no Buy Box eligibility for many categories + no advertising access.
- **Fix:** Register as **Professional Seller** from Day 1. Mandatory for Brand Registry, A+ Content, FBA at scale, Sponsored Ads.

### 1.2 GST Registration Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Listing without GST under the "books are exempt" assumption.
  - Books (HSN 4901, 4903) are GST-exempt at output, BUT Amazon **still requires GSTIN** to onboard as a seller in most categories. Exemption only applies to the product tax, not to the seller obligation.
- **Mistake 2:** Using personal PAN-linked GST when business is a Pvt Ltd / LLP / Proprietorship — creates Form 26AS mismatches.
- **Mistake 3:** Not registering GST in Karnataka (your warehouse state) AND in states where you hold FBA inventory → triggers "casual taxable person" issues.
- **Fix:**
  - Get GSTIN in Karnataka (principal place of business).
  - Add Amazon FBA warehouse addresses as **Additional Place of Business (APOB)** in your GST certificate. Without APOB, you cannot legally store inventory in FBA fulfilment centres outside Karnataka.
  - Most Phase-1 sellers skip APOB → notice from GST department within 6–12 months.

### 1.3 Brand Registry Skipped or Delayed
- **Mistake:** Launching without Amazon Brand Registry (ABR).
  - Without ABR: No A+ Content, no Brand Store, no Sponsored Brand Ads, no protection from hijackers, anyone can edit your listing.
- **Fix:** File **Trademark Application (TM-A)** for "SanskaTots" word mark + logo mark **immediately** if not done. Amazon now accepts "Trademark Applied" status (TM application number) for ABR enrolment in India — you don't have to wait for full registration. Use IP India e-filing, Class 16 (books, paper goods) + Class 28 (toys/educational products).
- **Cost:** ₹4,500/class for individual/MSME, ₹9,000/class otherwise.

### 1.4 Bank Account & Payment Mismatches
- **Mistake:** Bank account name ≠ Legal Entity Name on GST/PAN.
  - Causes payment holds of 14–90 days.
- **Fix:** Current account in **Deethya Enterprises** (exact legal name). Add as primary disbursement account before listing.

### 1.5 Wrong Default Tax Code
- **Mistake:** Setting default Product Tax Code (PTC) as A_GEN_TAX (18%) for books.
- **Fix:** Books use **PTC: A_GEN_NOTAX** or **A_BOOKS_GEN** (0% / exempt). Workbooks/activity books with significant non-paper content (velcro, felt) may attract 12% — get a CA opinion. Wrong PTC → you collect tax you shouldn't, customer disputes, refund chaos.

## SECTION 2 — CATEGORY & LISTING STRATEGY MISTAKES

### 2.1 Wrong Category / Browse Node
- **Mistake:** Listing busy books under "Toys & Games > Educational Toys" because they "feel like toys."
  - Books category has **lower referral fee (no fee for books under ₹250 in some sub-nodes)** vs. Toys (15.5%).
  - Toys requires **Compulsory BIS Certification (Toy Safety Order, 2020)** — without it, listings get suspended, inventory destroyed.
- **Fix:**
  - Velcro busy books with paper as primary material → **Books > Children's Books > Activity Books** (Browse Node: 4149710031).
  - Shloka board book → **Books > Children's Books > Religious**.
  - BhagavadGita for Kids → **Books > Children's Books > Mythology / Religious Stories**.
  - Pencil Control Tracing → **Books > Children's Books > Education & Reference > Workbooks**.
  - Learn Kannada → **Books > Language Learning > Kannada**.

### 2.2 ISBN Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Self-publishing without ISBN to "save time."
  - Books category on Amazon India **REQUIRES** ISBN for category approval. No ISBN = listing rejected or forced into "Toys" with all the BIS problems.
- **Mistake 2:** Buying ISBN from third-party resellers for ₹1,500–3,000.
  - ISBN in India is **FREE** from Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency (RRRNA), Ministry of Education. Apply at isbn.gov.in.
- **Mistake 3:** Using one ISBN for multiple book editions/sizes.
  - Each unique format (A4 vs A5, hardcover vs paperback, English vs Kannada) needs **separate ISBN**.
- **Fix:** Apply for **20+ ISBNs in one batch** now. Free, takes 2–4 weeks. You'll need them for Phase 2/3 anyway.

### 2.3 GTIN/EAN Exemption Not Applied For
- **Mistake:** Trying to list without barcode and getting blocked.
- **Fix:** For books, ISBN doubles as GTIN — Amazon auto-accepts. For non-book products (future felt boards), apply for **GTIN Exemption** in Seller Central → Inventory → Add a Product → Apply for GTIN Exemption (free, brand-owner only — needs ABR).

### 2.4 Title Optimization Mistakes (Most Common)
- **Mistake examples (real patterns from failed listings):**
  - ❌ "SanskaTots Busy Book" (too short, no keywords, no benefit)
  - ❌ "BEST BUSY BOOK FOR KIDS!!! BUY NOW MUST HAVE 2026 EDITION ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐" (banned characters, ALL CAPS, promotional)
  - ❌ "Activity book busy book quiet book toddler book preschool book" (keyword stuffing, no readability)
- **Amazon India Title Rules (2026):**
  - Max 200 characters (books category).
  - No promotional language ("best", "sale", "free shipping", "#1").
  - No special characters (~ ! * $ ? _ { } # < > | ; ^ ¬ ¦).
  - First letter of each word capitalized (no ALL CAPS except brand acronyms).
  - Format: **[Brand] + [Product Type] + [Key Feature/Age] + [Material/Size] + [Quantity]**
- **Correct example for SanskaTots:**
  > "SanskaTots All In One Busy Book for Toddlers Age 2-5 — Montessori Velcro Activity Book with 24 Learning Pages + 7 Reusable Cutting Sheets, A4 Size, Made in India"
  - 196 chars, brand-first, keywords natural, age range, USPs (Montessori, Velcro, reusable, Made in India), spec (A4, page count).

### 2.5 Bullet Points (Key Product Features) Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Generic features ("High Quality", "Made with Love", "For Kids").
- **Amazon India 2026 Rules:**
  - 5 bullets, 200 chars each (recommended 150–180 for mobile).
  - First 3 words in CAPS for scannability (e.g., "MONTESSORI METHOD:").
  - No emojis (Amazon strips them in books category).
  - No URLs, no contact info, no competitor mentions.
- **Winning bullet structure (use this):**
  1. **PROBLEM SOLVED** — "SCREEN-FREE LEARNING: Replace 2+ hours of daily screen time with hands-on Montessori activities that build focus, fine motor skills, and cognitive development in toddlers age 2-5."
  2. **CORE FEATURE** — "24 ACTIVITY PAGES + 7 CUTTING SHEETS: Numbers, alphabets, shapes, colors, animals, body parts, transport, fruits & 16 more themes — every page has reusable Velcro pieces stored in built-in pockets."
  3. **UNIQUE USP** — "MADE IN INDIA, BUILT TO LAST: 350 GSM laminated pages survive toddler tantrums, food spills & rough handling. Industrial-grade Velcro tested for 5,000+ peel cycles."
  4. **GIFT/CONVENIENCE** — "PERFECT GIFT FOR BIRTHDAYS, RAKHI, DIWALI: Premium box-ready packaging. Travel-friendly (fits in diaper bag). Engages kids during flights, restaurants, doctor visits."
  5. **TRUST/GUARANTEE** — "100% SATISFACTION GUARANTEE: 30-day replacement for any manufacturing defect. Designed by Indian parents, child-safety tested, non-toxic inks. WhatsApp support: [number]."

### 2.6 Description Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Pasting bullet points again into description, or leaving it blank.
- **Fix:** 1500–2000 char product description with:
  - Story (why you built this)
  - Detailed page-by-page or activity-by-activity breakdown
  - Comparison table (vs. competitors — without naming them)
  - Care instructions
  - HTML allowed: `<br>`, `<b>`, `<p>` (very limited — most HTML is stripped now)
- **Critical:** Once A+ Content is live, the description is hidden on desktop but **still shows on mobile** for ~40% of users. Don't neglect it.

### 2.7 Search Terms (Backend Keywords) Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Repeating words from title/bullets in backend (Amazon ignores duplicates → wasted space).
- **Mistake 2:** Using commas (Amazon parses by space, not comma — commas waste characters).
- **Mistake 3:** Stuffing competitor brand names ("not skillmatics not chu chu tv") — gets you suspended.
- **Mistake 4:** Ignoring **Hindi/regional language transliterations** — huge missed opportunity in India.
- **Fix (250-byte limit, ONE field, space-separated):**
  > montessori toddler busy quiet activity workbook preschool 2 3 4 5 year old bachon ki kitab sikhne wali velcro felt sensory autism gift birthday rakhi diwali kannada hindi marathi gujarati south indian made india homeschool playschool
- Use **250 bytes** exactly (not characters — special characters take more bytes).

### 2.8 Image Mistakes (Single Biggest Conversion Killer)
- **Amazon India Image Requirements (Books):**
  - Min 1000×1000 px (for zoom). Recommended **2000×2000 px**.
  - Main image: pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), product fills 85% of frame, no text/logos/badges.
  - Up to **9 images** allowed (use ALL 9).
- **Common mistakes:**
  - Only 1–3 images uploaded.
  - Lifestyle image as main (gets suppressed by Amazon).
  - Watermarks, "Best Seller" badges, "₹999 only" — all banned on main image.
  - Same image angle 7 times.
- **Winning 9-image stack for a busy book:**
  1. **Main:** Closed book, white background, 3D angle, full cover visible.
  2. **What's inside (collage):** 6–8 page thumbnails in a grid.
  3. **Lifestyle:** Toddler hands using the book (face partially visible — Amazon prefers).
  4. **Infographic:** "What your child learns" — colors, numbers, motor skills, vocabulary.
  5. **Comparison table:** Your book vs. typical activity book (pages, materials, reusability, price/page).
  6. **Material shot:** Close-up of Velcro, page thickness, lamination — with specs overlay.
  7. **Size reference:** Book next to a 1-litre bottle or a child's hand for scale.
  8. **Gift packaging:** Wrapped or boxed version (drives gift purchases — 35% of toy/book sales).
  9. **Video thumbnail (or 9th image):** "How to use" instructional shot.
- **Add a Product Video** (30–60 sec, MP4, max 1GB) — listings with video convert **80% higher** in Children's Books on Amazon India.

### 2.9 A+ Content Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Not creating A+ Content (requires ABR — see Section 1.3).
- **Mistake 2:** Treating A+ as a brochure (image-only with no scannable text).
- **Mistake 3:** Same A+ template across all 7 books (Amazon flags as low-quality).
- **Fix:**
  - Use **Premium A+ (A++)** if eligible (free if you have 5+ approved A+ submissions).
  - Modules to use: Comparison Chart (mandatory — compare your 7 books against each other), Brand Story Carousel, Image-Header-Text, Q&A module.
  - Mobile-first design — 70% of Amazon India traffic is mobile.

### 2.10 Variation (Parent-Child) Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Listing each book as standalone instead of using variation themes.
- **Fix:** For Phase 2+, group by:
  - **Theme:** "Busy Book — Choose Variant: All-In-One / Animals / Montessori"
  - Variations consolidate reviews under parent ASIN → faster review velocity → faster Best Seller Badge.
- **Caveat:** Books in India have limited variation themes — confirm with Seller Support before merging.

## SECTION 3 — PRICING & PROFITABILITY MISTAKES

### 3.1 Not Calculating Total Amazon Cost Before Pricing
- **The real cost of selling on Amazon India (Books, FBA):**

| Cost Component | % / Amount |
|---|---|
| Referral Fee (Books > ₹250) | 5–15% (varies by sub-category) |
| Closing Fee (FBA) | ₹15–55 per item by price band |
| Fulfilment Fee (FBA) | ₹38–95 by size band (Standard) |
| Storage Fee | ₹45/cubic foot/month + Long Term Storage Fee after 365 days |
| Removal Fee | ₹10–100 per unit |
| Pick & Pack Fee (FBA Easy Ship) | ₹30–50 |
| Weight Handling Fee | ₹38 (local), ₹45 (regional), ₹76 (national) per 500g |
| GST on Amazon Fees | 18% on every fee above |
| Promotional Spend (Coupons) | 5–10% typical |
| Advertising (PPC) | 15–30% of revenue (TACoS) for new products |
| Returns & RTO | 8–15% (kids category is high) |
| Payment Gateway Float | 7–14 days (working capital cost) |

- **Real example for All-In-One Busy Book at MRP ₹699:**
  - Referral fee (10%): ₹70
  - FBA fulfilment + closing: ₹70
  - Storage (avg): ₹8
  - GST on fees (18%): ₹27
  - PPC (20% TACoS): ₹140
  - Returns (10% × refund cost): ₹40
  - **Total Amazon-related cost: ₹355 (51% of MRP)**
  - **Net to seller: ₹344**
  - If COGS (printing + velcro + assembly + packaging) = ₹180, **gross profit = ₹164/unit (23%)**
- **Mistake:** Pricing at ₹399 thinking "low price = more sales" → losing ₹15/unit before scaling.
- **Fix:** Use **Amazon Revenue Calculator** (sellercentral.amazon.in/hz/fba/profitabilitycalculator) for EVERY SKU before listing.

### 3.2 MRP vs. Selling Price Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Setting MRP = Selling Price (no discount visible → lower CTR).
- **Mistake:** Setting MRP at ₹1,999 and selling at ₹399 (75% discount) — **Amazon India will reject as misleading MRP** under Legal Metrology Act + your listing gets the "MRP suspicious" suppression.
- **Fix:** Industry-honest MRP = 1.4–1.6× selling price. ₹699 selling → ₹999 MRP (30% off displayed). Get MRP printed on every product (legal requirement).

### 3.3 Coupons & Deals Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Running ₹100-off coupon on Day 1 → Amazon may not honor it because you have no "reference price" history yet.
- **Fix:** First 30 days: build pricing history at MRP. Day 31+: introduce 5–10% coupon. Day 60+: apply for Lightning Deals / Best Deals (requires sales history + ratings).

### 3.4 Subscribe & Save / Buy More Save More
- **Books are not eligible for S&S** but you can run "Buy 2 Save 5%" promotions — drives multi-book purchases (your bundle play).
- **Mistake:** Not creating a **Virtual Bundle** (ABR feature) of all 3 Velcro busy books → leaving ₹500–800 AOV uplift on the table.

## SECTION 4 — INVENTORY, FBA & LOGISTICS MISTAKES

### 4.1 Wrong Fulfilment Choice
| Option | When to Use | Mistake |
|---|---|---|
| **FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon)** | Steady-selling SKUs, Tier-1 cities focus | Sending too much inventory upfront — storage fees eat profit |
| **Easy Ship (Seller-Fulfilled, Amazon picks up)** | Phase 1 testing, low volume | Stocking-out due to 24hr pickup delays |
| **Self-Ship** | Custom/handmade, B2B orders | Losing Buy Box, no Prime badge, lower conversion |
| **SmartConnect / Multi-Channel Fulfilment** | If selling on Flipkart/Meesho too | Not using it → managing 3 inventory pools |

- **Recommendation for SanskaTots Phase 1 (June 2026):**
  - **Months 1–2:** Easy Ship for all 7 SKUs (test demand, no storage commitment).
  - **Month 3+:** Move top 3 sellers to FBA (typically the 3 Velcro busy books).
  - **Always keep 30 units in self-ship inventory** for B2B/bulk/website orders.

### 4.2 FBA Inventory Sizing Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Sending 1000 units to FBA on Day 1 → 500 units sit for 12 months → Long Term Storage Fee (LTSF) of ₹150/cubic foot/month kicks in.
- **Mistake 2:** Sending 50 units → stock-out in week 2 → losing rank, losing "Frequently Bought Together" placements, restart from zero.
- **Fix:** Send **30 days of forecasted inventory + 50% safety stock**. For a new launch, conservative estimate: **80–120 units per SKU per month**.

### 4.3 IOR (Inbound Shipment) Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Wrong FNSKU labels, wrong box dimensions, missing pallet config → Amazon rejects shipment, charges ₹150–500 per unit re-labeling fee.
- **Fix:**
  - Use Amazon's **Partnered Carrier Program** for first 3 shipments (Amazon handles labels).
  - Print FNSKU stickers using thermal printer (DYMO LabelWriter or Zebra ZD220).
  - Maximum 30kg per box, 25 boxes per appointment.
  - Always book **inbound appointment** — walk-in deliveries are penalized ₹2,000+.

### 4.4 Storage Limits
- New sellers get **only 50 cubic feet** of storage in FBA. Books are dense → 50 cu.ft = ~3,000 books (large) or 8,000 (small).
- **Mistake:** Not knowing your IPI (Inventory Performance Index) score → storage limits get cut.
- **Fix:** Keep IPI > 400. Track in Seller Central > Inventory > Inventory Performance.

### 4.5 Returns Handling Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Not opting for "Returnless Refund" for low-cost items (<₹300) → Amazon charges return logistics + you get a damaged unit back.
- **Mistake:** Not inspecting FBA returns → Amazon can re-list "Customer Damaged" inventory as "New" → reviews tank.
- **Fix:**
  - Set **Return Window: 10 days** for books (default 30 is too long).
  - Enable **Customer Service by Amazon (CSBA)** — Amazon handles refunds for you (small fee).
  - For FBA: Schedule monthly **removal orders** for returned inventory → inspect → re-grade or destroy.

## SECTION 5 — ADVERTISING (PPC) MISTAKES — THE BIGGEST MONEY DRAIN

### 5.1 No Ad Strategy = No Sales (in 2026)
- **Reality check:** 73% of all Amazon India sales come from sponsored placements. Organic-only = invisible.

### 5.2 Common PPC Mistakes
1. **Running Auto Campaigns only for 6+ months** → you never learn which keywords convert.
2. **No negative keywords** → wasting ad spend on irrelevant searches ("free busy book pdf", "busy book template download").
3. **Bidding on broad single words** ("books", "kids") → ₹50+ CPC, no conversions.
4. **Ignoring SP (Sponsored Products) → SB (Sponsored Brands) → SD (Sponsored Display) funnel.**
5. **Running ads with no review velocity** (0–5 reviews) → wasted spend, conversion <2%.

### 5.3 Recommended PPC Structure (First 90 Days)

**Phase 1 — Discovery (Days 1–14):**
- **Auto Campaign** — ₹300/day budget, default bid ₹8.
- Goal: harvest keywords. Don't worry about ACoS.
- Run **4 separate auto campaigns** per ASIN (Close Match, Loose Match, Substitutes, Complements) — Amazon's structure.

**Phase 2 — Manual Targeting (Days 15–45):**
- Pull Search Term Report → identify keywords with 2+ orders.
- Move these into **Manual Exact Match** campaigns with higher bids (₹15–25).
- Add zero-conversion, high-spend keywords as **Negative Exact** in Auto.

**Phase 3 — Scale (Days 46–90):**
- Launch **Sponsored Brands** (requires ABR) — headline ad with all 7 books → drives Brand Store traffic.
- Launch **Sponsored Display** retargeting — ads to people who viewed but didn't buy.
- Launch **Product Targeting** — bid on competitor ASINs (Skillmatics busy book, Wonder House activity books).

### 5.4 Budget Mistakes
- **Mistake:** ₹100/day "to test" → Amazon spends it in 4 hours, you lose data.
- **Fix:** Minimum viable ad budget for new product on Amazon India = **₹500/day per SKU for first 30 days** = ₹15,000/SKU/month. For 7 SKUs = ₹1.05L/month minimum.
- **Realistic ACoS expectations:**
  - Month 1: 60–100% (loss-leader, building reviews & rank)
  - Month 3: 30–50%
  - Month 6: 15–25% (target steady state)
  - Month 12: 10–18% (mature)

### 5.5 Targeting Mistakes Specific to Children's Books in India
- **Bid on regional terms:** "kannada book for kids", "marathi balgeet book", "tamil kids book".
- **Festive surge:** Bid 2–3× higher in Aug-Oct (Rakhi, Onam, Navratri, Diwali) — gift season drives 40% of annual children's book sales.
- **Don't waste bids on:** "free pdf", "download", "homework helper" — non-buyers.

## SECTION 6 — REVIEW & RATINGS MISTAKES (CATEGORY-CRITICAL)

### 6.1 Why Reviews Make or Break Children's Books
- Amazon India shoppers in Books category check reviews **3.2× more** than other categories.
- Below 20 reviews + below 4.3 stars = **death zone** (organic conversion <1%).
- Above 100 reviews + 4.5+ stars = automatic Amazon's Choice candidate.

### 6.2 Review Generation Mistakes (Most Get You Banned)
- ❌ Asking friends/family to review (Amazon detects via IP, address, payment method links).
- ❌ Offering refunds in exchange for reviews ("Review Manipulation" — permanent ban).
- ❌ Inserting "Please leave 5 stars" cards in packaging (illegal since 2017 update).
- ❌ Buying reviews from Telegram/Fiverr groups (Amazon's ML detects within 30 days).

### 6.3 Legitimate Review Strategies
1. **Amazon Vine** (requires ABR + new ASIN <30 reviews):
   - Send up to **30 free units** to Amazon's invited reviewers.
   - Cost: ₹0 (was ₹16,000 — now FREE in India as of 2024).
   - Get 8–15 honest reviews in 30 days. **DO THIS DAY 1.**
2. **Request a Review button** in Seller Central:
   - Manually click for every order 5–30 days post-delivery.
   - Or automate via tools like **FeedbackWhiz, Sellesta, AMZ Helper** (₹1,500–4,000/month).
3. **Insert Cards (Legal Version):**
   - "Scan QR for free coloring printable" → leads to your WhatsApp → asks for review **only if** they had a great experience (NPS-style).
   - Never demand a positive review.
4. **Influencer Seeding** (separate from Amazon):
   - Send to 50 mom influencers → they post on Insta → traffic comes to Amazon listing → buys → leaves organic review.

### 6.4 Negative Review Management
- **Mistake:** Ignoring 1-star reviews.
- **Fix:**
  - Respond publicly within 24 hours: empathetic, solution-offering.
  - Use **"Report Abuse"** for reviews violating policy (profanity, competitor sabotage, off-topic).
  - For genuine product complaints: contact buyer via Buyer-Seller Messaging → resolve → politely ask for review update.

## SECTION 7 — PRODUCT QUALITY & POLICY MISTAKES

### 7.1 BIS / Safety Compliance (CRITICAL for Children's Products)
- **Books category** = mostly exempt from BIS.
- **BUT** if your busy book has:
  - **Detachable Velcro pieces small enough to be a choking hazard (<3cm)** → potentially classified as toy → BIS toy safety rules apply.
  - Felt, plastic embellishments → toy classification risk.
- **Fix:**
  - Add prominent label: **"Recommended Age: 3+. Adult supervision required. Small parts — choking hazard for children under 3."**
  - Keep all detachable pieces **>3cm in any dimension**.
  - Keep documentation of materials safety (non-toxic ink certificates from your printer).

### 7.2 Legal Metrology / Packaging Rules
- **Mandatory on every product label:**
  - MRP (₹ symbol, Inclusive of all taxes)
  - Net Quantity (e.g., "1 N" or "1 Book + 7 Cutting Sheets")
  - Manufacturer Name & Full Address (Deethya Enterprises, Karnataka)
  - Country of Origin: **Made in India**
  - Customer Care: Email + Phone (mandatory)
  - Date of Manufacture (Month/Year)
  - For imported items: Importer details
- **Mistake:** Missing any of the above = ₹10,000–25,000 fine per SKU under Legal Metrology Act + Amazon listing suppression.

### 7.3 Packaging Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Shipping books in flimsy plastic poly bags → 30%+ damage in transit → 4-star average max.
- **Mistake 2:** Over-packaging with thermocol → returns for "excessive plastic" + BBMP/Bangalore plastic ban issues.
- **Fix for SanskaTots:**
  - Corrugated 5-ply box (₹18–25/unit).
  - Inside: book wrapped in single-layer kraft paper + 2 cardboard inserts.
  - Branded thank-you card with QR to WhatsApp community.
  - Include: 1 free sticker sheet (cost ₹3) → drives "above expectation" 5-star reviews.

### 7.4 Restricted/Prohibited Content Risks
- **Mistake:** Using copyrighted characters (Disney, Chhota Bheem, Doraemon images) in book pages → instant takedown + IP strike.
- **Mistake:** Religious imagery without proper attribution → community complaints → listing suspension.
- **Fix:** All artwork must be original or properly licensed. Keep copyright/license certificates ready for Amazon's IP verification team.

## SECTION 8 — OPERATIONAL & FINANCIAL MISTAKES

### 8.1 Cash Flow Mistakes
- **Reality:** Amazon India payment cycle = **7 days for Easy Ship, 14 days for FBA** (was 7, changed in 2023).
- **Mistake:** Spending revenue before disbursement clears → unable to restock → stock-out → rank loss.
- **Fix:** Maintain **45 days of operating cash** as buffer. Use **Amazon Lending** (invitation-only after 3 months of consistent sales) for working capital — better rates than NBFCs.

### 8.2 Reconciliation Mistakes
- **Mistake:** Trusting Amazon's payment reports without reconciliation.
  - Amazon overcharges sellers by **2–7% on average** due to: wrong weight category billing, missed reimbursements for lost/damaged FBA inventory, returned items charged twice, etc.
- **Fix:**
  - Use **GETIDA, Refully, SellerApp, or Helium 10 Refund Genie** (₹0–₹3,000/month — many work on % of recovered amount).
  - Manually file claims via Seller Central > Help > Contact Us > FBA Issue every 15 days.
  - Typical recovery: ₹5,000–₹50,000/month for a sub-₹10L revenue seller.

### 8.3 Tax Compliance Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Not filing **GSTR-1, GSTR-3B monthly** even when books are exempt → late filing penalties (₹50/day).
- **Mistake 2:** Not claiming **Input Tax Credit (ITC)** on Amazon fees (you pay 18% GST on every fee — fully claimable).
- **Mistake 3:** Not filing **TCS (Tax Collected at Source) reconciliation** — Amazon deducts 1% TCS on every sale, you must claim it via GSTR-2A → many sellers leave ₹20K-2L/year unclaimed.
- **Fix:** Hire a CA familiar with e-commerce (₹3,000–8,000/month). Don't DIY this.

### 8.4 Account Health Metrics — The Silent Killers
| Metric | Target | Suspension Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Order Defect Rate (ODR) | <1% | >1% for 60 days |
| Late Shipment Rate (Self-Ship) | <4% | >4% for 30 days |
| Pre-Fulfilment Cancellation Rate | <2.5% | >2.5% |
| Valid Tracking Rate | >95% | <95% |
| Customer Service Dissatisfaction Rate | <25% | >25% |
| Policy Violations | 0 | Any major violation |
