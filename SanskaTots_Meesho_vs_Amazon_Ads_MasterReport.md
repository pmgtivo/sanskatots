### 3.4 ACoS Reality Check for SanskaTots

**ACoS = Ad Spend / Ad Revenue × 100**

Your Break-Even ACoS is the percentage of revenue you can spend on ads and still not lose money. Calculate it:

```
Example: All-In-One Busy Book

Selling Price on Amazon:         ₹599
Amazon Referral Fee (0% books):  ₹0 (books are exempt)
Amazon Closing Fee:              ₹7-30 (weight based)
Amazon Easy Ship / FBA:          ₹60-90
Packaging:                       ₹15-25
Manufacturing Cost:              ₹150-200 (estimate)
                                 ─────────
Total Cost:                      ~₹232-345
Gross Margin:                    ₹254-367
Gross Margin %:                  42-61%

Break-Even ACoS:                 42-61%
```

**Target ACoS should be BELOW your Break-Even ACoS.**

If your manufacturing cost is high (>₹200), your Break-Even ACoS drops to ~42%. An ACoS of 50% means you are losing money on every ad sale. Sounds bad — but it's an investment in rank, velocity, and reviews. Accept this for 60-90 days. Then optimize.

> **Hard Truth:** At ₹249 (Pencil Tracing Book), your margin is dangerously thin. Referral + FBA + packaging can eat ₹100-130 of your ₹249. Ad spend on this ASIN needs to be extremely targeted or you will actively lose money on every sale. This book should NOT be your primary ad focus.

### 4.3 What Actually Drives Meesho Sales (Organic Levers)

This is where Meesho is fundamentally different from Amazon. On Meesho, these organic factors matter MORE than ads:

**1. Product Pricing (Most Critical Factor)**
- Meesho's algorithm heavily rewards competitive pricing
- A product priced 10-15% lower than competitors gets 3-4x more organic visibility
- Your All-In-One Busy Book at ₹599 is competing with ₹299-399 "similar looking" books
- Consider: Is ₹499 on Meesho your real play? Test pricing aggressively.

**2. Reseller Ratings (The Meesho Review System)**
- Reseller satisfaction drives your "supplier rating" which directly impacts visibility
- Pack orders perfectly. No defects. Use proper bubble wrap.
- A single batch of poorly packed books = bad reseller rating = algorithm demotion = 0 sales

**3. Return Rate / Cancellation Rate**
- Keep return rate under 5% or Meesho will shadow-restrict your catalog
- COD is ~65% on Meesho. Some COD orders are fake. Build this RTO (Return to Origin) cost into your price.

**4. Category Tags and Product Attributes**
- Meesho's search is attribute-driven. Fill EVERY attribute field:
  - Age group, Language, Number of pages, Activity type, Material, Colour
  - Missing attributes = invisible in filtered searches (which are the majority of purchase-intent searches)

**5. Catalogue Completeness**
- List ALL 7 books. Catalog depth signals a reliable supplier. More SKUs = more surface area.
- Create bundles (Busy Book + Tracing Book = ₹799 combo) — Meesho resellers LOVE combo packs because they can charge more and appear to give value.

## PART 7 — THE 10 THINGS THAT WILL KILL YOUR AD SPEND (AVOID THESE)

### 7.1 Amazon Ad Killers

1. **Advertising before you have 5+ reviews.** Cold traffic with 0 reviews converts at 2-4%. You are paying for clicks that will not buy. Get 5 reviews first via Early Reviewer Program or giving out free copies for honest reviews.

2. **Running only AUTO campaigns without converting to manual.** Auto campaigns are data collectors. After Week 2-3, pull search term reports, find keywords that converted, move them to Manual-Exact. If you never do this step, you bleed money forever.

3. **Setting one campaign for all 7 books.** When one book underperforms, it pulls budget from your winners. Separate campaigns for each ASIN. Always.

4. **Ignoring negative keywords.** Auto campaigns will target "free activity book for kids," "activity book PDF download," "activity book template." These people will NEVER buy. Add 50+ negative keywords in Week 1.
   - Negative list to add from Day 1:
     - free, PDF, download, template, printable, DIY, how to make, worksheet free, coloring pages free

5. **Stopping ads the moment you get sales.** New sellers see 10 sales in week 3, panic about ACoS, pause all ads. The ranking falls immediately. Sales disappear. You restart. Rinse and repeat. This is the hamster wheel of failure.

6. **Not using the Search Term Report weekly.** Amazon shows you EXACTLY which search terms triggered your ad and whether they converted. If you are not reviewing this weekly, you are flying blind.

7. **Bidding the same for all keywords.** A keyword like "bhagavad gita book for kids" might convert at 25% (very intent-specific). A keyword like "books for kids" might convert at 3%. The second keyword deserves a ₹5 bid, not ₹30. Use dynamic bidding and keyword-level bid management.

8. **Launching during a dead period.** July-mid August is actually a reasonable period in your category (back to school prep). But avoid heavy ad launches in October (Navratri, Dussehra) if your listings aren't optimized — high traffic that doesn't convert wastes budget and signals poor relevancy to the algorithm.

9. **Not testing prices.** Price is the single biggest conversion factor. Test ₹549 vs ₹599 on your All-In-One Busy Book for 2 weeks each. The difference in conversion can be 30-40% — which dramatically changes your ACoS.

10. **Not setting daily budget caps correctly.** If you set ₹500/day and Amazon spends ₹480 on one bad campaign in the morning, you have ₹20 left for your good campaigns. Set SEPARATE budgets per campaign. Cap low performers. Keep high performers well-funded.

### 7.2 Meesho Specific Mistakes

1. **Listing all books at the same price without testing.** Meesho's algorithm rewards price adjustments. Drop a book by ₹20-30, watch ranking improve overnight.

2. **Ignoring RTO and not building it into price.** If 20% of your Meesho orders return, and each return costs you ₹80 in shipping both ways, that's ₹16 cost per order on average. Build this into every Meesho price.

3. **Not using WhatsApp captions in your Meesho descriptions.** Your resellers are your sales force. Make their job easy with ready-to-share captions. They will sell more. You earn more.

4. **Inconsistent dispatch time.** Meesho penalizes late dispatch severely. A supplier with <24 hour dispatch gets 2x the algorithm visibility vs 48-72 hour dispatch. If you're doing this from home — set up a dispatch system or hire a part-time packer.
