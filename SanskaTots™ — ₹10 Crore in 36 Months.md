## PHASE 1: DOMINATE AMAZON INDIA (Months 1–10 | July 2026 – April 2027)

**Phase Target: ₹12–18 lakh/month by Month 10**

Amazon is your launchpad. You need to become the #1 search result for "busy book for kids," "activity book for toddlers," "write and wipe book" before you do anything else.

### 1.1 — Amazon PPC: Go From Zero to ₹15 Lakh/Month Revenue

Start spending on ads TODAY. Not tomorrow. Today.

| Campaign Type | Daily Budget | Target ACoS | Purpose |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Sponsored Products — Auto (all 7) | ₹200/ASIN = ₹1,400/day | 35% (learning phase) | Keyword discovery |
| Sponsored Products — Manual (exact) | ₹300/ASIN = ₹2,100/day | 20–25% | Revenue driver |
| Sponsored Brands (Video) | ₹1,000/day | 15–20% | Brand awareness + conversion |
| Sponsored Display (Retargeting) | ₹500/day | 25–30% | Cart abandoners, competitor audiences |
| **Total Daily Ad Spend** | **₹5,000/day = ₹1.5 lakh/month** | | |

**Expected return at 4x ROAS:** ₹1.5L spend → ₹6L ad-attributed revenue + ₹4–6L organic = **₹10–12 lakh/month by Month 4–5.**

**PPC Rules (non-negotiable):**
- Harvest keywords from Auto campaigns weekly → move winners to Manual Exact
- Negative match irrelevant terms aggressively (no spend on "coloring book" or "CBSE")
- Bid on competitor names: "Skillmatics," "Einstein Box," "Shumee," "Intellikit"
- Run Sponsored Brand Video ads — they have 3x higher CTR than static ads in kids' category
- Every ₹1 of ad spend must return ₹3.5+ in revenue within 60 days or kill the campaign

### 1.2 — Reviews: The #1 Ranking Factor

No reviews = no organic rank = permanent dependence on ads. You need velocity.

**Target: 50+ reviews on each ASIN within 90 days. 200+ on top 2 ASINs by Month 6.**

| Tactic | Cost | Expected Reviews |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| Amazon Vine Program (all 7 ASINs) | ₹14,000/ASIN = ₹98,000 one-time | 15–30 reviews per ASIN |
| Insert card in packaging (QR → review page) | ₹2/card | 5–8% conversion rate on deliveries |
| WhatsApp follow-up Day 5 post-delivery | Free (manual) or ₹2,000/mo (tool) | 10–15% response rate |
| Instagram DM to buyers who tag you | Free | 2–3 reviews/week |
| Request via Amazon "Request a Review" button | Free | Do it for EVERY order, every day |

**Never incentivize reviews with discounts/cash.** Amazon will suspend your account. The insert card should say: *"Loved it? Share your experience on Amazon. It helps other parents discover us."*

## WHAT WILL KILL YOUR GROWTH (Avoid These)

1. **Underspending on ads in Month 1–6.** You MUST spend ₹1.5–2 lakh/month on Amazon PPC from day 1. This is not an expense — it's buying market share. Every day without ads is a day your competitor gets the customer.

2. **Ignoring reviews.** A product with 10 reviews CANNOT compete with one having 200 reviews, no matter how good your product is. Vine + insert cards + follow-ups — every single day.

3. **Staying Amazon-only beyond Month 8.** Amazon takes 20–30% of your revenue in fees. D2C + Flipkart diversify your risk AND improve margins.

4. **Not expanding SKUs.** 7 books cannot generate ₹10 crore. You need 20–30 SKUs targeting different ages, languages, and price points.

5. **Running out of stock during peak season.** If you're out of stock during Diwali or back-to-school, you lose rank, reviews, and momentum. Always stock 60–90 days ahead.

6. **Trying to do everything yourself.** By Month 6, you MUST have at least 1–2 people helping. A performance marketer and an ops person free you to focus on product and strategy.

7. **Ignoring offline retail after ₹3–4 crore.** E-commerce alone caps around ₹5–6 crore. The jump from ₹5cr to ₹10cr comes from offline distribution.
