# Krishna's Story Sequence — 8 Key Scenes
### Images For Little Ones Aged 3–5 Years

---

**Scene 1: The Magic Birth**
One stormy night, baby Krishna was born! The sky lit up with stars. His father carried him safely across a big river. Baby Krishna smiled — and the water made way for them.

---

**Scene 2: Butter Baby Krishna**
Baby Krishna LOVED butter. He climbed pots, snuck into kitchens, and shared stolen butter with his monkey friends. Mummy Yashoda always found empty pots — and a sticky, smiling Krishna!

---

**Scene 3: Yashoda Sees the Universe**
One day, Mummy Yashoda asked Krishna to open his mouth. She saw the whole sky, all the stars, and every world inside his tiny mouth. Krishna was no ordinary baby!

---

**Scene 4: Krishna and the Snake Kaliya**
A big, scary snake lived in the river and hurt everyone. Little Krishna jumped into the water, danced on the snake's head, and said, "Stop being mean!" The snake promised to be good and swam away.

---

**Scene 5: Lifting Govardhan Hill**
Rain fell for seven days — very heavy rain! People were scared. Krishna lifted a big mountain on his little finger like an umbrella. Everyone stood underneath, safe and dry.

---

**Scene 6: Holi — The Colour Festival**
Krishna loved colours! He played Holi with his friends, throwing red, yellow, and blue colours everywhere. Everyone laughed, ran, and got totally colourful. It was the most fun day of the year!

---

**Scene 7: Krishna Defeats the Bad King Kansa**
There was a bad king called Kansa. He was mean and made everyone scared and sad. When Krishna grew up, he went to Kansa's kingdom and challenged him. Krishna was brave and defeated Kansa. He freed all the good people and brought his real mummy and papa home. The whole kingdom danced with joy!
> **Lesson:** Being brave and doing what is right always wins over being mean and cruel.

---

**Scene 8: Krishna — Best Friend of the Pandavas**
Krishna had five special friends called the Pandavas. When bad people tried to take away everything the Pandavas had — their home, their family, everything — Krishna never left their side. He did not fight for them, but he always guided them, loved them, and said: *"I am with you. Do not be afraid."* He was their charioteer, their guide, and their best friend.
> **Lesson:** A true friend stands with you in your hardest times — not just in fun times. Be that friend to someone.
