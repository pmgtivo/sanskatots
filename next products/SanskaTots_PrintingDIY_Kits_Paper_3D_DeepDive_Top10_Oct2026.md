# ⚡ THE ONE-PAGE VERDICT

**1. Compliance changes the shortlist — this is the most important finding in the report.**
- BIS's own licensing manual for IS 9873 (Appendix I) **lists origami, colouring kits, "perforated and cut-out toys", puppets, "dress-me" books and "stickers and albums with play function" as toy categories** [B1].
- So "it's only paper" does **not** keep a product out of BIS. The **truly no-licence lanes** are:
  - **📗 Book lane:** a *bound* book (ISBN, pictures/worksheets principal), whose output is a picture, a worksheet or a written book — **HSN 4903/4901, Nil GST**.
  - **🖼️ Décor & stationery lane:** craft kits whose *finished item* is decorative or stationery, not a plaything (journals, wall art, cards, festival ornaments) — IS 9873-1 explicitly excludes *"hobby and craft items in which the finished item is not primarily of play value"* and *"holiday decorations primarily intended for ornamental purposes"* [B2].
  - **🏫 School lane:** teaching aids sold to schools (HSN 9023), not toys.
- **🔴 Red (needs BIS):** puzzles, puppets, masks, play figures, fidgets, 3D-printed flexi animals, paper planes/launchers, board/escape-room games.

**2. Demand is real and big in the lanes you can enter.**
- Paper + 3D DIY on Amazon.in alone moves **≥ 1,17,500 units/month (≥ ₹5.8 Cr/month GMV)** in our sample. The Green-lane segments alone are **≥ 36,450 units/month (≥ ₹1.67 Cr/month)**.
- **Journals & scrapbook kits are the single biggest Green-lane segment**: ≥ 10,100 units/month on Amazon.in, the #1 printing segment on Meesho (29,569 reviews) and a top segment on Flipkart (12,634 ratings).

**3. India has "home-turf" formats where it out-buys the US — and they are printing formats.** Share of category vs the US (1.0 = same share):
- **Mandala / folk-art kits 5.6×**, **cut-&-paste / scissor skills 5.0×**, **sticker-by-number 4.5×**, school science/projects 4.3×, **card-making 4.2×**, **scrapbook/journal 2.6×**.
- These are built on Indian parenting habits (school projects, fine-motor prep for LKG/UKG, Indian art) — Chinese generic imports don't speak to them.

**4. And there are US trends that haven't landed in India yet (timing plays).**
- **Paper/Taba squishy kits** (US ≥ 82,200/month; India ≈ 0 DIY kits, but Indians already search "paper squishy making kit", "taba squishy making kit"); reusable sticker books (0.42×); water-reveal books (0.46×); paint-your-own figurines (0.38×); stencils (0.15×).
- Most of these are 🟡/🔴 for BIS, so they are **wildcards**, not Top-10 picks (§6.4).

**5. Two funded leaders own the middle — and leave clear holes.**
- **Imagimake** (FY25 ₹90 Cr; Pidilite investing) and **Skillmatics** (FY26 ₹659 Cr, 87% abroad) sell ₹300–₹900 single kits.
- Holes they leave: **₹149–₹449 single kits**, **₹99–₹179 per-kit return-gift packs**, **Indian-culture and regional-language content**, **Meesho** (almost absent), and **schools**.
- Many winners are new: **92 Amazon.in printing/3D products sell ≥ 200/month with < 300 reviews** — e.g. My Artsy Stash journal kit (2,000+/month, 169 reviews), BeginBird Sticker-by-Number (1,000+/month, 208 reviews), Qlect Busy Binder (3,000+/month, 286 reviews). **A new brand can break in fast.**

**6. China: the paper lane has weak import protection, so you must win on content, speed and cost — not on law.**
- Chinese books/stickers enter at ~5–10% duty, Nil/18% IGST and no BIS; toys pay 70% duty + BIS [C1–C4].
- Chinese FOB for a sticker/activity book is **$0.30–1.10 (₹29–105)**; a gem/sticker mosaic kit at $1.20 FOB lands in India at **≈ ₹153 if declared as paper** vs **≈ ₹324–349 if declared as a toy** (incl. BIS licence cost; at ₹95/$) [C5].
- **Your own BOM for a comparable printed kit is ≈ ₹60–90 at 2,000 units (est.)** — cheaper than a paper-classified import, with 1–2 week reprints vs their 6–10 week cycle.

**7. 3D printing: cheap to start (₹26k printer), but don't lead with it.** Kids' 3D-printed figures/fidgets are toys (🔴). Use 3D printing for **festival décor (paint-your-own diya/Ganesha), Indic-script stencils, and teaching models for schools/ATL labs**.

- **Value-add zone (your zone):** sticker-by-number, scissor-skills, journals with content, folk art, story makers — Chinese supply exists, but **Indian retail is ₹449–₹849 and is driven by content and brand, not paper cost.**

## 3.5 How SanskaTots beats Chinese imports — 10 moves

1. **Sell content, not paper.** Indian heritage, wildlife, festivals, CBSE/state syllabus, Kannada/Hindi/Tamil editions — things a Yiwu factory won't design.
2. **Price where imports can't follow:** ₹249–₹449 single books and ₹99–₹179 per kit in packs. A legally-imported toy-classified kit lands at ₹324–349 before marketplace fees.
3. **Use speed as a weapon:** print 300–500 units, reprint in 1–2 weeks, refresh themes monthly. Importers are locked into 2,000-unit MOQs and 6–10 week cycles.
4. **Build series, not SKUs:** one mechanic × 6–12 themes (Water WOW! has 41, Foil Fun 23). Series = cross-sell + brand search + defensibility.
5. **Personalise** (child's name on the cover, "publish your child's story" print service) — impossible for importers.
6. **Own the channels importers can't:** schools (GST invoice, teacher guides, GeM), corporate gifting, return-gift bulk orders.
7. **Make quality visible:** non-toxic inks, sticker adhesive that holds, sanded/creased cut lines, honest age bands, a spare-sticker sheet. Leaders' complaint themes are "stickers won't stick", "missing pieces", "too little material" [D19].
8. **Make "Made in India" visible:** marketplaces now show a country-of-origin filter (from 1 Jul 2026) [B21].
9. **Don't chase commodity formats** (magic water, plain scratch paper, gem stickers) except as add-ons inside premium sets.
10. **Re-check duty and enforcement every Budget** — toy imports are rising again (+18% Apr–Aug 2026), and enforcement against mis-declaration is increasing [C1, C6].

# ⚖️ PART 4 — COMPLIANCE: STAYING OUT OF BIS (verified Oct 2026)

> ⚠ = secondary source / not verified on a primary document. **Research, not legal advice.** Get a written opinion from a BIS consultant (₹15–30k) and your CA *before* printing packaging.

## 4.1 The rules that decide everything

- **Toys QCO scope:** products "designed or clearly intended… for use in play by children under 14" [B12].
- **IS 9873-1 exclusions that help you** (verified on the 2012 edition; secondary sources say the 2025 edition is substantially unchanged ⚠) [B2]:
  - "Model kits, hobby and craft items in which the finished item is **not primarily of play value**"
  - "Holiday decorations that are **primarily intended for ornamental purposes**"
  - "Puzzles having more than 500 pieces or without a picture, for specialists"
  - Collectibles not intended for under-14s
- **There is no exclusion for books, art materials, stationery or educational aids** — books escape only because they are not "play" products [B2].
- **BIS product manual, Appendix I — toy categories that name paper formats** [B1]:

| BIS category | Start age | Hits which of your ideas |
|---|---|---|
| #105 Books with pop-ups, **hidden pictures** and **"dress-me"** | 2+ | Water-reveal books, paper-doll/dress-up books |
| #107 **Stickers and albums "with play function"** | 4+ | Reusable play-scene sticker books |
| #117 **Perforated and cut-out toys** | 4+ | Press-out play figures (not cut-and-paste worksheets) |
| #120 **Folding crafts – origami** | 7+ | Origami kits |
| #121 **Technical models to assemble** | 8+ | 3D paper/cardboard models for kids |
| #122/#123 Colouring & painting kits incl. **stencils and crayons** | — | Colouring *kits* (not colouring *books*) |
| #124 Painting sets with water-based paints | 4+ | Window art, paint-your-own figurines for kids |
| #125 Drawing sets | — | Scratch-art *kits* |
| #131 **Puppets** | 3+ | Puppet/mask making |

## 4.2 How to keep each product in a safe lane (design rules)

1. **Bind it.** A bound book with pictures as the principal interest is 4903 (US CBP rulings classify activity books with stickers and paint-with-water books as 4903) [B7–B9]. Loose sheets in a box drift toward 4911/9503 — the only Indian ruling found (MP AAR) classed an *unbound* learning kit as 4901 at 5%, not 4903 [B15].
2. **Make the output a picture, a worksheet, a written book, a card or a décor piece** — never a play object. US CBP ruling K83774 classified a "press-out and build" cardboard helicopter *book* as a constructional toy [B10].
3. **Age-grade honestly upward where it is genuine:** mandala, folk art, journals, sticker-by-number (adult edition), 3D models (12+ display) are naturally 10+/teen/adult products.
4. **Use book/stationery words, not toy words** in titles and bullets: "activity book", "art book", "journal kit", "wall-art kit" — not "toy", "game", "play set".
5. **Avoid toy add-ons inside Green kits:** no LEDs, no candles, no projectiles, no plush, no balloons.
6. **Stationery add-ons are fine:** there is no BIS order for crayons, pencils, watercolours, glue, scissors or googly eyes [B3]. If a product ever *does* become a toy, those add-ons must then pass IS 9873-3 heavy-metal tests — so **buy crayons/paints that come with IS 9873-3 or EN 71-3 test reports anyway.**
7. **Get one written BIS opinion covering the whole range** (list each SKU, photos, age band, listing category) — it is cheap insurance.

## 4.4 GST & fees in practice

- **Nil-rated books can't claim input-tax credit.** The 18% GST on paper (4802/4810, raised from 12% to 18% in GST 2.0) and on Amazon fees becomes a **cost** — model it into COGS (Part 8) [B4].
- **A book + crayons/scissors sold as one set** is classified by its essential character (the book), but there's a risk of "mixed supply" treatment at the highest rate under s.8(b) CGST Act — **get your CA's view before launch** [B7, B8].
- **Amazon.in referral fees** [B5]:

| Category | Referral |
|---|---|
| **Books** | 0% ≤ ₹250 · 2% ₹251–500 · 4% ₹501–1,000 · 13.5% > ₹1,000 |
| **Office Products – Arts & Crafts / Craft Materials** | 0% ≤ ₹1,000 · 5% above |
| Toys – Art & Craft (needs BIS) | 0% ≤ ₹1,000 · 11.5% above |

- **ISBN:** free in India (Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency) [B13]. Eligible for printed books and text-led mixed media; **not for games**. An ISBN helps a genuine book list in Books; it does **not** change HSN or BIS status.
- **EPR (plastic packaging):** micro/small brand owners are reportedly exempt from targets under the 2024–25 amendments but may still need CPCB registration ⚠ [B14]. Design plastic out: paper sleeves, kraft boxes, paper void-fill.

## 7.2 Process flow (paper lines)

```
Design (series template) → digital proof → 20-copy pilot → kid/parent test (5 families)
   → fix → print (sheets) → kiss-cut / die-cut / perforate → bind
   → kit (count by weight) → QC → pack (sleeve/box) → barcode/ISBN → ship to FBA / Easy Ship / Meesho
```

## 7.3 Your content system (the real moat)

- **One series template per line** (cover grid, page grid, instruction style, age badge, "Did you know?" box). New themes = new art only.
- **An Indian illustration library** (animals, monuments, festivals, foods, vehicles, people in Indian dress) re-used across #1, #3, #4, #5, #9, #10 — design once, sell many times.
- **Bilingual layer** (English + Kannada first, then Hindi/Tamil) on #2, #3, #5 — no importer and almost no Indian brand does this.
- **ISBN per book title** (free) and **copyright registration** for hero artwork.
- **Instruction booklets written for a 7-year-old and a tired parent** — Klutz's lesson.

## 7.4 QC checklist (printed DIY specific)

| Check | Standard |
|---|---|
| Sticker adhesion | Stuck to the art page, still holding after 7 days; no edge lift |
| Kiss-cut depth | Sticker peels cleanly; liner not cut through |
| Registration | Polygon stickers within ±0.5 mm of their outlines (#4) |
| Perforation | Page tears out cleanly in one pull, no ripping into art |
| Colour | Matches the master proof (critical for sticker-by-number) |
| Piece count | Check by weight per kit; **add one spare sticker/ephemera sheet** (kills "missing pieces" reviews) |
| Edges | No sharp board edges on models/boards |
| Labels | Age band, Legal Metrology block, ISBN/barcode, "Made in India" |
| Packaging | Survives a 1 m drop and a 20 kg stack test |

## 7.5 Packaging = shipping cost (the silent margin killer)

- Amazon charges shipping on the **higher of actual or volumetric weight (L×B×H ÷ 5,000)**.
- An A4 book in a sleeve (30 × 21 × 1.5 cm) = **189 g volumetric** → stays in the lowest slab.
- The same contents in a 30 × 21 × 5 cm box = **630 g volumetric** → jumps a slab (≈ ₹20 more per order).
- **Rule: ship books flat in kraft sleeves; use boxes only for gift tiers (₹699+).**

# 🧠 PART 11 — 30 THINGS A FOUNDER MUST KNOW ABOUT THIS CATEGORY

### A. Market & customer
1. **"DIY kit" is four different buyers:** moms of 2–6 (fine motor, school readiness), tween/teen girls 8–16 (journals, foil, sticker art — bought by moms/aunts as gifts), adults (mandala, sticker-by-number, colouring — the "kidult" wave), and schools/teachers.
2. **India already buys printing-DIY at scale:** ≥ 1,17,500 units/month on Amazon.in alone in our sample — and that's a floor.
3. **India's home-turf formats** (mandala/folk art, cut-and-paste, sticker-by-number, card-making, journals, school projects) are where Indian content beats imports.
4. **US trends arrive in India 1–3 years later** (water-reveal, reusable stickers, squishy kits, paint-your-own). Watch Amazon.com best sellers monthly.
5. **Gifting drives this category:** birthdays (return gifts), Christmas/New Year, festivals, Children's Day. Design every line with a gift tier and a pack tier.
6. **Summer holidays (Apr–May) and the festive season (Aug–Nov) are the peaks; Feb–Mar exams are the trough.**

### B. Product & content
7. **The format is the brand** — one mechanic, many themes. Plan 4–12 themes per line before you launch theme #1.
8. **Mess-free wins.** Stickers, foil, dot stickers, water pens — not glue and paint — for under-8s.
9. **Instructions and age bands are product features**, not packaging text.
10. **Sticker quality makes or breaks reviews:** adhesive strength, liner release, kiss-cut depth, registration. Test before every new paper batch.
11. **Always add one spare sheet** — "missing pieces" is the #1 complaint across the category.
12. **Indian content is your moat, but it has to be beautiful.** Imagimake's Indian Art Forms sells at ₹1,149 because it looks premium.
13. **Bilingual (English + Kannada/Hindi) is a white space** — none of the leaders do it.
14. **Make learning visible on the box** ("builds scissor control", "learn 20 Indian animals", "Madhubani history inside").
15. **Flat formats ship cheaper.** Volumetric weight decides your shipping slab.

### C. Compliance & money
16. **Paper is not automatically BIS-free.** BIS lists origami, cut-out toys, puppets, dress-me books and play stickers as toy categories.
17. **Your safe lanes:** bound books (4903, Nil GST), stationery/décor kits, school teaching aids.
18. **Never print "toy", "game" or "play set" on a Green-lane SKU**, and age-grade honestly (10+, 12+, adults where true).
19. **An ISBN helps a real book; it doesn't make a toy into a book.**
20. **Nil-GST books can't claim input credit** — price it in; it's still your best-margin lane.
21. **Journal kits can be 18% or Nil** depending on classification — a ₹40/unit question for your CA.
22. **Shipping and ads are bigger costs than paper** on ₹199–₹499 items — sets and flat packaging fix this.
23. **Chinese paper-lane imports pay ~5–10% duty and no BIS** — your edge is content, speed and cost, not the law.
24. **3D printing is cheap to start but most kids' 3D products are toys**, and most popular designs need paid commercial licences.

### D. Channels
25. **Amazon = discovery + price premium** (Books lane referral 0–4%; Office Products 0% ≤ ₹1,000).
26. **Meesho = volume at ₹100–₹370** — packs of 2–4; several Top-10 formats have zero competition there today.
27. **Flipkart over-indexes on journals/scrapbook, quilling and stencils** — list Desi Diaries there first.
28. **Schools are mandated buyers** (CBSE art-integrated projects, ATLs) — B2B margins ≈ 45% with no ads or returns.
29. **Your website** carries no referral or closing fee — use it for bundles, personalisation and "publish my child's book".

### E. Brand
30. **Sub-brand the series under SanskaTots** (Snip & Learn, Dot-a-Lot, Sticker Art India, Desi Diaries, Kala Kits, Story Maker, Build India) — they become search terms, and series storefronts lift every SKU.

# ⚠️ PART 12 — RISKS & KILL CRITERIA

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| BIS treats a Green SKU as a toy (e.g. dot-sticker or foil book) | Medium | High | Written BIS opinion before printing; bound book format; honest age bands; no toy words; keep a BIS application path ready for Amber items |
| GST classification dispute (4903 Nil vs 4911/4823 18%) | Medium | Medium | CA opinion per SKU; consistent invoices and listing categories |
| Sticker/die-cut quality problems → bad reviews | Medium | High | Registration plotter; 7-day adhesion test; spare sheet; QC by weight |
| Skillmatics/Imagimake drop prices or copy Indian themes | Medium | Medium | Series depth, bilingual, Meesho, schools, personalisation |
| Chinese copycats of your designs in 60–90 days | High | Medium | Copyright registration, Brand Registry, fast theme refresh, content they can't copy |
| Ad costs stay > 25% TACoS | Medium | High | Bundles, reels/UGC, Meesho organic, B2B mix |
| Seasonality (Feb–Mar dip) | High | Low | B2B and summer-camp pipeline built in Feb |
| 3D IP infringement | Low (if own designs) | High | Own designs only, or active commercial licences |
| Over-expansion (30+ SKUs before winners are known) | Medium | Medium | Waves; keep/kill rules below |

**Kill / keep rules (apply every 30 days from launch):**
- **Kill a SKU** if, after 60 days and ≥ ₹5,000 ad spend, it sells < 30 units/month at ≥ 4.0★. Don't reprint it.
- **Fix-or-kill a line** if all its SKUs together sell < 100 units/month after 90 days.
- **Pause ads** on any SKU above 40% ACoS for 3 straight weeks; fix the main image/price first.
- **Scale a SKU** (offset run + steel-rule die) once it sells ≥ 150 units/month for 2 months at ≥ 4.3★.
- **Stop any line immediately** if a BIS or GST authority questions its classification — then reformat (book/décor) or take the licence.
