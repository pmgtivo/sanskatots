## KEY CLARIFICATIONS FOR FUTURE REFERENCE

### **What is a "Velcro Activity Book" / "Busy Book"?**

A Velcro activity book (also called a busy book or quiet book) consists of **two components**:

1. **Main Book Pages** (Spiral-bound)
   - Printed pages with activity stations
   - Velcro **hook** areas affixed at specific locations
   - Each page presents a learning challenge or activity zone
   - Child interacts with Velcro-attached pieces

2. **Cutting Pages** (Separate, Die-Cut)
   - Single-sided printed pages with die-cut shapes/pieces
   - Velcro **loop** affixed to back of each cut-out piece
   - Child removes pieces and "places" them onto hook areas in the main book
   - Creates reusable, interactive learning experience

### **What is NOT a Busy Book?**

- Books with just coloring pages
- Books with just tracing pages
- Books with just stories (no interactive elements)
- Books with glue-and-stick activities (permanent, use-once)
- Books with pop-up or paper engineering (rigid, fragile)

### **Why Velcro Hook + Loop?**

- **Reusable:** Pieces can be attached/detached infinite times
- **Safe:** No glue, no mess, appropriate for toddlers (age 1.5+)
- **Engagement:** Tactile feedback and satisfying "click" of Velcro attachment
- **Durability:** High-quality Velcro maintains grip even after 100+ uses
- **Special Needs Friendly:** Perfect for children with fine motor delays or sensory needs
