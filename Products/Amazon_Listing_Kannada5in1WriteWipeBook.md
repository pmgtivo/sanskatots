**Background:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` — mandatory per Amazon TOS. No text overlay allowed.

**Why this works:** On Amazon mobile, users see a 200×200px thumbnail before they click. If your 3D illustration is bold enough and the Kannada script is visible, you own that click from Kannada-speaking parents before they even read your title.
