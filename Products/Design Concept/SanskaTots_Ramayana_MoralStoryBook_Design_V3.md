## V3 STORY PRINCIPLE

Every story page has **three text layers** working together:

| Layer | What It Is | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Story Context Line** | 1 sentence at the top of the page (italicised, small) | Connects this story to the one before it — so reading feels like one flowing Ramayana |
| **Panel Caption** | 2 short read-aloud sentences per panel (15–20 words max) | Parent reads aloud → child follows the actual Ramayana story |
| **Speech Bubble** | 1 key character line in the panel (max 8 words) | Gives character voice, adds emotion and personality |
| **Modern Life Moral** | 1–2 sentences at the bottom (lotus border box) | Connects the ancient lesson to the child's life today |

> **For Parents:** Each panel caption is written to be read aloud, simply and warmly. Read one panel at a time, point at the picture, then read the next. Your child is hearing the real Ramayana — one page at a time.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### Core Principle: Every Page Is a Mini Story
Each of the 22 story pages is a **complete, self-contained mini-story** told in 4–6 comic panels. The panel captions together form a read-aloud story — not just labels. A parent reads the captions; the illustrations carry the emotion; the moral box closes every story with a life lesson.

### Target Audience
- **Primary (Kids 2–5):** Cannot read. Panels guide the eye left → right, top → bottom. Big expressive faces, clear actions, bright colors. The illustration tells the story visually even before a caption is read.
- **Secondary (Parents):** Want to teach the actual Ramayana — one clear story page at a time — with a simple modern lesson they can talk about together.

### Design Rules

| Rule | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **80% Illustration / 20% Text** | Each panel has 2 short caption sentences + 1 speech bubble. Title + Context + Moral box. |
| **Comic Panel Grid** | 4 panels = 2×2 grid. 5 panels = 2+3 or 3+2. 6 panels = 2×3 or 3×2 grid. |
| **Big Faces, Big Emotions** | All characters drawn in **chibi/kawaii** style — oversized heads, huge eyes, exaggerated expressions |
| **No Scary Imagery** | Ravana = grumpy/silly villain. Battles = cartoon action. No blood, no fear. |
| **Warm Indian Palette** | Saffron, golden yellow, sky blue, forest green, lotus pink — zero black or grey |
| **Consistent Characters** | Same chibi style for all 16 named characters across all 22 pages |
| **Read-Aloud Caption** | 2 sentences, max 20 words total, simple vocabulary (Grade 1 level) |
| **Speech Bubbles** | Key character lines in round speech bubbles inside panels — max 8 words |
| **Panel Numbering** | Subtle ① ② ③ numbers in corner of each panel to guide reading order |
| **Story Context** | A 1-line italicised banner below the story title connecting it to the prior story |

## V3 PRODUCTION NOTES FOR ILLUSTRATORS

### What Changed in V3 (vs V2)

| V2 | V3 |
|---|---|
| Panel caption = 5–8 word title label | Panel caption = 2 read-aloud sentences (parent reads aloud) |
| No story context connector | Each page has a 1-line Story Context that connects the narrative |
| Illustrations carried all story weight | Illustrations + captions together tell a complete mini-story |
| Child heard a label | Child hears a full flowing read-aloud Ramayana story |

### Text Formatting in Panels
- Each panel caption area: **min 20mm height** at bottom of panel (taller than V2)
- Caption font: Nunito Regular, 10pt, dark charcoal (#333333), line-height 1.4
- Max 2 sentences per panel caption. No bullet points.
- First sentence = what is happening (story). Second sentence = what it means/causes.
- Speech bubble max 8 words in Bangers or Comic Sans, always inside the panel above the caption strip

### Story Context Line
- Appears below the Story Title in *italic Nunito Light*, 9pt, warm grey (#888888)
- Max 1 sentence. Connects this story to the prior page chronologically.
- Box or rule separator between context line and the panel grid

### Illustrator Key Notes
1. **Character Continuity:** Create a master character sheet for all 16 characters FIRST — use it for every single panel.
2. **Panel Emotion Rule:** Every panel has ONE primary emotion. Never mix or crowd. The illustration matches the caption's emotional beat.
3. **Page 15, Panel 5:** The THREE GOLDEN STRIPES from Rama's fingers on the squirrel's back MUST be clearly visible — it is the origin-story reveal.
4. **Page 21, Panels 3–4:** The diya-lighting sequence is the most warm and iconic visual in the whole book. Full golden glow, every detail of Ayodhya at night.
5. **Page 22, Panel 3:** Most technically complex panel — all 21 story moments must be visible as tiny glowing vignettes inside Hanuman's open chest. Create all 21 vignette thumbnails first.
6. **Page 7, Panel 4:** The CONTRAST of Rama's simple wooden sandals on the grand golden throne is the key image of this page. Make the contrast very visible and dramatic.
7. **Page 8, Panel 5:** Jatayu's final expression — noble and at peace, not sad or defeated. This is a hero's moment. Golden light.

### Software and Technical Specs
- **Format:** Adobe Illustrator (vector) preferred for print quality
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI minimum, CMYK color mode
- **Bleed:** 3mm all sides | Safe Zone: 5mm inside trim
- **Trim size:** 210mm × 148mm (A5 Landscape)
- **Panel borders:** 2pt rounded-corner saffron border (#FF6B2B)
- **Inter-panel gutters:** 4mm | Caption strip height: 20mm (bottom of each panel)
- **Rich black for type:** C:40 M:30 Y:30 K:100
- **Moral box:** Full-width, 14mm height, saffron (#FF6B2B) background, white bold text

## PARENT READ-ALOUD TIPS (inside front or back flap / introductory panel)

> **Dear Parent,**
>
> This book is designed to be read TOGETHER — your voice brings these stories to life!
>
> **How to use it:**
> - Point at each panel illustration first and let your child look at the picture.
> - Then read the caption aloud, clearly and warmly.
> - Do the character voices! Ravana gets a grumpy deep voice. Hanuman gets an excited, bouncy voice. Sita gets a calm, clear voice. Rama gets a gentle, steady voice.
> - After every page, talk about the 🪷 Moral box together: *"This means that when you go to school today..."*
> - The Story Context line at the top of each page connects all 22 stories into one big flowing Ramayana narrative.
>
> **By the time you finish this book, your child will know:**
> - All the main characters of the Ramayana
> - The complete story in Kanda (chapter) order
> - 22 life lessons they can recognise in their own daily lives
>
> *Happy reading. The best bedtime stories are the ones that live forever.*
> — **SanskaTots**
