**How to read the numbers**
- **Search Frequency Rank:** lower = searched more. It is a rank, not a volume.
- **Click share / conversion share (top product):** the share of clicks and sales the #1 product gets for that term. A low share means no one owns the term.
- Data includes terms down to rank ~322,000.

**Limits**
- No **Books** or **Office Products** category file was downloaded. Terms whose top clicks land in Books/Office Products can be missing from the Toys file.
- One month only (October, a Diwali/festive month). Re-check in February.

## 5. The Toys vs Office Products question (and what the data adds)

| Search type | Where the shoppers click |
|---|---|
| diy kit for kids, craft kit, art & craft kits | **Home → Toys → Office Products** |
| paper craft kit, kids scissors, origami kit | **Office Products** first |
| gift for 4/5 year old, return gift, finger puppets, toys for N year old | **Toys** |
| activity book for N year old | **Books** |

- **Demand sits in two lanes.** The DIY/craft-kit terms (your best, rank 26k) reach shoppers through Home/Toys/Office Products. The gift-by-age, return-gift and puppet terms are Toys-lane.
- **This does not solve the BIS question.** BIS depends on whether the product is a toy (puppets, mask, play value), not on the browse node. The earlier recommendation stands: **get a written BIS opinion.** I have not confirmed that listing in Office Products avoids BIS scrutiny.
- If your BIS opinion says the kit is safe as a craft kit, the title above works in any lane. If it must be treated as a toy, you can add "toy" words later.

## 7. Method notes
- Terms were de-duplicated across the six files and filtered by regex for each cluster. Obvious off-target terms (adult kits, resin, drones, jewellery, plush, wooden houses, teen/12+ ages) were removed. The CSV lists 281 terms with rank, tier, top-3 brands, click share, conversion share, category and recommended placement.
- Demand tiers: A ≤ 30k · B 30–100k · C 100–200k · D > 200k. Competition: Open < 10% · Moderate 10–25% · Locked > 25% top-1 click share.
- Placement labels in the CSV are a first pass. Treat "Title / Item Highlights" as a shortlist, not a final decision.
- This analysis shows what shoppers search and click. It doesn't show what *your* listing will rank for.
