## Notes on Ranking Strategy

1. **Title:** Don't change the primary title in the first 2–3 weeks after listing — Meesho's search algorithm needs a stable title to build ranking signal. Test alternates only after 200+ views on the primary.
2. **Reviews first 30 days matter most** — ask your first 20 buyers (family/friends via reseller network) to review with photos; Meesho ranks partly on review velocity early on.
3. **"Busy book" + "mythology" is your white-space combination** — no competitor on Meesho currently ranks for both terms together, so lead with it in title, description opening line, and tags.
4. **Avoid unrelated high-volume terms** from your list like "mythology greek", "mythology timeless tales", "mythology board game" (Western/board-game intent) — they'll bring clicks that don't convert and can hurt your listing's relevance score over time.
5. **Update specs once printed** — confirm final page count and sticker count match what's on the physical book before going live; mismatched specs cause returns and hurt ratings.
