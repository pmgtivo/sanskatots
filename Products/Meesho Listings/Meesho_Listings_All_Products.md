## Pro Tips for Meesho Success
- **Images win on Meesho:** Resellers share product images on WhatsApp. Use lifestyle images (child holding/using the book) alongside clean white-background images.
- **Price competitively:** Meesho buyers are price-conscious. Ensure your Meesho price stays below your Amazon price.
- **Ship fast:** Meesho resellers lose trust if orders are delayed. Commit to 1–2 day dispatch.
- **Offer a combo:** Create a combo listing (e.g., "All In One + Montessori Busy Book Combo") at a bundled price — combos perform very well on Meesho.
- **Respond to reseller queries fast:** Resellers ask product questions before sharing — reply within 2 hours.
