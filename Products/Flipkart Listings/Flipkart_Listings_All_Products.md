> ## HOW FLIPKART LISTINGS ARE DIFFERENT — READ THIS FIRST
>
> Flipkart is a **direct-to-consumer marketplace** (like Amazon), NOT a reseller platform like Meesho. Buyers here are parents making a direct purchase decision, so listings must be as sales-driven and detailed as Amazon — but the platform has different technical rules:
>
> | Rule | Detail |
> |---|---|
> | **Title length** | Max 200 characters. Flipkart's algorithm favors clean, readable titles over pure keyword-stuffing — front-load Brand + Product Name + 2–3 killer features. |
> | **No HTML** | Flipkart strips HTML tags in most listing flows. Use plain text with line breaks and `•` bullets — never `<b>`, `<ul>`, `<li>`. |
> | **Key Features field** | A separate short bullet block (5–8 bullets) shown ABOVE the description on the product page. This is the most-read section after the title/images — treat it like Amazon's 5 bullets. |
> | **Description field** | Long-form plain text, ~2,000–4,000 characters. No bold/italics — persuasion has to come from word choice and structure, not formatting. |
> | **Specifications** | Flipkart requires structured category attributes (Brand, Binding, Language, Number of Pages, Ideal For/Age Group, Included Components, etc.) selected from dropdowns/fields — these directly power Flipkart's on-site filters, so accuracy here drives discoverability. |
> | **No backend "hidden keywords" field** | Unlike Amazon, Flipkart does not give sellers a private keyword field. **All SEO keywords must be naturally woven into the Title, Key Features, and Description**, and separately loaded into **Flipkart Ads (Performance Max / Sponsored Listing) campaign keywords** for paid discovery. The keyword lists below serve both purposes. |
> | **Mandatory legal fields** | Country of Origin: **India**. Manufacturer/Packer/Importer name & address: **Deethya Enterprises**. Net Quantity: **1 (Book + Pen + Duster set, where applicable)**. These are Legal Metrology–mandated and Flipkart will not publish a listing without them. |
> | **No superlatives/claims policy** | Same as Amazon — avoid "best," "No.1," "sale," "guaranteed," medical/developmental claims that sound clinical. Use "designed to help," "supports," not "cures" or "guarantees." |
> | **Category placement** | List under **Books > Children's & Young Adult Books > Activity/Educational Books** (or **Baby Care > Learning & Development Toys** for the Velcro busy books, since Flipkart's Toys category often converts better for interactive/reusable products — test both if allowed). |
