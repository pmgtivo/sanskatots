## THE SCRIPT

### [0:00 – 0:03] — PATTERN INTERRUPT HOOK *(The Scroll Stopper)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**
> Close-up shot of laptop screen — 20+ browser tabs visible, all open simultaneously. Nagarathna's hand is frantically scrolling through them. The screen is a chaotic mess of printer reviews, Amazon product pages, YouTube videos, and WhatsApp notifications.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (bold, white, center):**
> *"I am launching on Amazon in 30 days. ONE decision completely broke me."*

**Nagarathna says (to camera, slightly breathless, genuine):**
> *"Yaar, I actually thought I was ready."*


---

### [0:03 – 0:15] — MICRO-STORY HOOK *(The Setup)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**
> Cut to a proud, warm shot of Nagarathna holding up the Sanskatots books one by one — All In One Busy Book, BhagavadGita for Kids, Learn Kannada. She fans them out like a card dealer. Then — hard cut back to confused face.

**Nagarathna says (excited, then deflated):**
> But then I realised — I am not ready at all. I forgot one tiny detail that is actually kind of important for selling on Amazon.
> Hi I am Nagarathna, founder of SanskaTots. We make educational activity books for kids. We are launching on Amazon in June. 
> to launch designs are done, printing is in progress, everything is set.
>
> And then we realised — 'what printer are we using for the labels?'

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**
> Freeze frame on her face. Slow digital zoom in.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> *"...labels?"*

---

### [0:15 – 0:35] — RELATABLE PAIN HOOK *(The Overwhelm)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**
> Fast-cut montage, almost chaotic: browser tabs flying open, YouTube video thumbnails (printer reviews), Amazon product pages, WhatsApp group notifications piling up, her scrolling frantically, writing notes, crossing them out. Energy: controlled chaos.

**Nagarathna says (faster pace, slightly exasperated, talking like she's explaining to a friend):**
> *"Simple question, right?*
>
> *I opened Google.*
> *First result says — 'Thermal Printer — no ink, runs forever, PERFECT for Amazon shipping labels.'*
> *Okay, that is it, I thought.*
>
> *Second result says — 'No no no. Inkjet printer — versatile, you can print everything, better quality.'*
>
> *Then I went to YouTube. Same fight happening there also.*
> *Fifty different people, sixty different opinions.*
>
> *Three hours later — multiple tabs open. Zero decision made."*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (appears mid-sentence, stays):**
> *"3 hours. Multiple tabs. 0 decision."*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**
> She slowly closes the laptop with both hands. Leans back. Stares at the ceiling. Then looks back at camera — tired, but slightly amused at herself.

---

### [0:35 – 0:48] — COMMUNITY CTA *(The Engagement Bomb)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**
> She sits up straight. Looks directly into the camera. Warm, genuine, slightly dramatic smile. She holds both hands out in a "you tell me" gesture.

**Nagarathna says (direct, warm, like talking to her best friend):**
> *"So thought I would ask you all for help. Because I am actually paralyzed by this decision. I have no idea which one to choose."*
>
> *Please drop a comment which printer would suit me for ecommerce business*
