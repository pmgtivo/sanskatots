### 📝 Quick Production Notes
- **Length:** ~40 seconds (cut the Proof part to hit 30 sec if needed)
- **On-screen text hook (first 3 sec):** *"POV: Your kid is stuck to the phone like Fevicol 📱😂"*
- **B-roll flow:**
  - Child glued to phone (the problem) →
  - You taking phone away → kid doing full "filmy" drama →
  - You looking tired/guilty →
  - Showing the busy book →
  - Child happily playing with the book (the happy transformation)
- **Comedy tip:** Lean into the *filmy drama* and *Fevicol* lines — exaggerate the acting, that's what makes desi audiences laugh and share!
- **Tone shift:** Funny → warm → excited. That emotional + comedy mix is what gets saves and shares.
- **Audio:** Use a trending desi comedy or "transformation" sound for max reach.
