## 🔴 Problem-Based Reels
*(Stop-the-scroll hooks — parents feel seen, product is the answer)*

1. **"My kid won't sit still for even 5 minutes"** — Show the chaos, then reveal how a Busy Book creates independent focus time.
2. **"Screen time guilt is real"** — A mom staring at her phone while the toddler watches YouTube → flip to a Busy Book moment instead.
3. **"Restaurant with a toddler = nightmare"** — Meltdown at a table → one Busy Book in the bag fixes everything.
4. **"My child has zero attention span"** — Highlight why flashcards and worksheets fail toddlers, show hands-on velcro activities.
5. **"Your child can't hold a pencil and school is 6 months away"** — The fine motor skills panic every parent feels in April.
6. **"Bored at home but won't touch any toy"** — Toys that get ignored vs. an activity book they keep coming back to.
7. **"My kid asks 'what do I do?' every 10 minutes"** — WFH parent getting interrupted constantly → Busy Book = uninterrupted work time.
8. **"Flight with a 3-year-old and no plan"** — Panic at the airport gate → compact Busy Book as the travel essential nobody talks about.
9. **"Teaching ABCs and they just don't get it"** — Frustration of rote learning vs. learning Kannada through a 5-in-1 activity format.
10. **"Every toy breaks in 2 days"** — Cheap plastic toys vs. a reusable velcro activity book that lasts for years.

5. **"Age 2 to 4: the most critical window for motor development"** — Why this phase matters and what parents can do right now.
6. **"Why regional language matters for a child's identity"** — The case for teaching Kannada early, tied to the Learn Kannada 5-in-1 book.
7. **"BhagavadGita for kids — why it's not too early"** — Values, stories, and character-building for toddlers explained simply.
8. **"3 ways to improve pencil grip at home"** — Practical demo using the Pencil Control Tracing Level 1 book.
9. **"How sloka recitation boosts memory in young children"** — The neuroscience angle, tied to the Shloka Board Book.
10. **"Why play IS learning — the science in 60 seconds"** — Research-backed explanation with a product demo woven in.

## 🏭 Behind the Scenes — Manufacturing
*(Builds credibility, shows craft and care — perfect trust-building content)*

1. **"How a page in our Busy Book is made"** — From design sketch to printed laminated page, step by step.
2. **"The velcro quality test no one talks about"** — Show how each velcro piece is checked before it goes in the book.
3. **"Inside our printing process"** — Printer setup, colour calibration, paper selection — demystify it.
4. **"Cutting pages — precision matters"** — Close-up of how cutting practice pages are designed with safety in mind.
5. **"From design to delivery — one book's journey"** — Full behind-the-scenes arc in 60 seconds.
6. **"Why we chose this paper weight"** — The decision behind child-safe, durable paper for toddler hands.
7. **"Packing an order by hand"** — Show the care that goes into wrapping, labelling, and dispatching each order.
8. **"What a rejected page looks like"** — Quality control moment — pages that don't make the cut and why.
9. **"The night before a launch — what it really looks like"** — Raw and real footage of stock prep, stickers, last-minute checks.
10. **"How we designed the Shloka Board Book cover"** — Design process, colour choices, typography — the story behind the look.
