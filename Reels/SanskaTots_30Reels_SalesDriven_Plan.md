# SanskaTots™ — 30 Reels Plan: Fun × Emotion × SALES
### Deethya Enterprises | May–June 2026
**Goal:** Every reel must end with a parent WANTING to buy. Sales is the ultimatum.

---

> **The Rule:** No reel exists just to "build brand." Every single reel has a sales trigger — either direct (link in bio / Amazon) or indirect (creates desire so strong that they search for you). Fun and emotions are the VEHICLE. The product is the DESTINATION.

---

## SALES PSYCHOLOGY FRAMEWORK

Each reel uses one of these 5 emotional sales triggers:

| Trigger | What It Does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **GUILT → RELIEF** | Makes parent feel the guilt they already carry, then shows the product as the fix | Screen time guilt → Velcro book saves the day |
| **FEAR OF MISSING OUT** | Shows other kids/parents already using it, creates urgency | "Every mom in my building ordered one" |
| **PRIDE / IDENTITY** | Makes buying = being a good Indian parent | "My kid knows who Hanuman is at age 3" |
| **COMPARISON SHOCK** | Shows the gap between cheap/generic and SanskaTots quality | Side-by-side teardown |
| **CHILD JOY** | Nothing sells harder than a happy child — parent's brain screams "I want that for MY kid" | Kid's genuine reaction to the book |

# RULES FOR MAXIMUM SALES

1. **Every reel caption MUST have "Link in bio" or "Comment [WORD] for the link"** — no exceptions
2. **First 3 seconds decide everything** — if the hook doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters
3. **Show the PRODUCT in every reel** — even emotional reels must have the book visible for at least 3 seconds
4. **Post between 8-9 PM IST** — peak Instagram time for Indian moms (kids are asleep, scrolling time)
5. **Reply to EVERY comment within 1 hour** — algorithm rewards engagement velocity
6. **Pin a comment on every reel** with the Amazon link or a purchase CTA
7. **Use 2-3 hashtags only** — #SanskaTots #BusyBooksIndia #ScreenFreeKids (Instagram penalizes hashtag stuffing now)
8. **Share every reel to Stories** with a poll or question sticker ("Would your kid love this? YES/ABSOLUTELY 😂")
9. **DM everyone who comments "link" or "interested"** within 5 minutes — speed = sales
10. **Repost top-performing reels after 2 weeks** with a different caption — most followers won't see it the first time
