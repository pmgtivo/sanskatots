# REEL SCRIPT — "I Built Our Website at 2AM While My Baby Slept"

**Content Pillar:** Emotional / Founder Story
**Emotional Trigger:** GUILT → RELIEF (audience feels the sacrifice, then feels proud of her)
**Content Phase:** Phase A — Hype & Tease
**Hook Type:** Micro-Story (raw, personal, no setup)
**Duration:** 50–58 seconds
**Voice:** English — warm, tired, real. No polish. Spoken like a whisper-confession.

---

## THE SCRIPT

---

### [0:00 – 0:04] — HOOK *(Emotional Gut-Punch)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Dark room. Low light. Shot of a laptop screen glowing in the dark — a half-built website visible on screen. A baby monitor sits next to the keyboard. You look into the camera — tired eyes, messy hair, quiet smile. No intro. No setup. Just truth.

**Nagarathna says (soft, direct, like a confession):**
> *"I built our entire website... at 2 in the morning. While my baby slept."*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> *2AM. Baby sleeping. Laptop open. Building a dream.*

---

### [0:04 – 0:14] — THE REALITY *(Why She Had No Choice)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Cut to daylight — you holding your baby with one arm, phone in the other hand, scrolling through Shopify pricing. Tired but functional. This is the duality of a mompreneur.

**Nagarathna says (honest, a little raw):**
> *"I am bootstrapped. Every rupee I spend on an agency or a platform subscription is a rupee I don't have. So I made a decision — I will build it myself."*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> *No agency. No budget. Just me and 5 years of engineering experience.*

---

### [0:14 – 0:30] — THE STRUGGLE *(Where It Gets Real)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Night-time montage — fast cuts. Baby sleeping in the crib. You at the laptop, screen bright in the dark. Cup of cold chai. A coding error on screen. You rubbing your eyes. Typing again. Clock showing 1:47AM. Then 2:30AM.

**Nagarathna says (voice slightly cracked, emotional):**
> *"Every night after my baby fell asleep — that was my window. I would open the laptop and work until 2AM. Sometimes longer.*
> *And at 6 in the morning, before I could even fully wake up — my baby needed to be fed.*
> *There was no 'rest.' There was only: baby, then website building. Website building, then baby."*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> *Work hours: 11PM – 2AM*
> *Wake-up call: 6AM — baby feed time*
> *Sleep? What is that?*

---

### [0:30 – 0:44] — THE WIN *(She Did It)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Slow reveal — you turning the laptop screen toward the camera. The SanskaTots website is live. Beautiful. Real. Yours. Your face breaks into a genuine, tired, proud smile. Maybe even a little teary.

**Nagarathna says (quiet pride, voice warm):**
> *"And somehow... I pulled it off.*
> *Our website is live. Built by a mom. Coded in the midnight hours."*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> *I did it. ✅*
> *SanskaTots website — LIVE.*

---

### [0:44 – 0:55] — ACTION RELEASE *(The Ask)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> You look directly into the camera — calm, warm, genuine. Not selling. Just asking.

**Nagarathna says (soft, personal):**
> *"I poured everything I had into this. I just have one small request — Please go check it out, and tell me honestly in the comments: what do you think?*
> *Your feedback means a lot to me"*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> *Link in bio 🔗 — Tell me what you think in the comments 👇*
> *Every comment read personally. Every one.*

---
