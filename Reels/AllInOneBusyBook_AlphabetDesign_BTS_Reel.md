# Reel Script — All In One Busy Book: Alphabet Design Process
**File:** AllInOneBusyBook_AlphabetDesign_BTS_Reel.md

---

## Reel Concept

- **Content pillar:** Behind the Scenes + Product Showcase
- **Phase:** C (Post-Launch)
- **Emotional sales trigger:** GUILT → RELIEF (screen time)
- **Hook type:** Visual Hook — velcro rip + 3D alphabet page reveal
- **Core message:** Every letter in this book was designed to out-compete a phone
- **Duration:** 45–60 seconds
- **Voice:** English (casual Indian)
- **Audio:** ASMR velcro sound at open, then voiceover throughout

---

## Script

---

### Level 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0–3s)

**[VISUAL]**
Close-up on hands opening the All In One Busy Book to the Alphabet Velcro page.
Slow velcro rip sound as the letter "A" piece lifts off.
Cut to: the full 3D illustrated page — rich colours, Apple and Ant illustrations popping off the page.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT — bold, centre]**
> "This is why my child hasn't asked for my phone in 3 days."

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"Let me show you exactly how we designed this."*

---

### Level 2 — Curiosity Gap (3–8s)

**[VISUAL]**
Slow zoom into one letter tile — showing the texture, the 3D depth, the two word slots beneath it.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
> "Every letter has a secret."

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"We didn't just put an A on a page and call it done. There's a reason a child will stare at this for 20 minutes straight."*

---

### Level 3 — Relatable Tension (8–18s)

**[VISUAL]**
Quick cut: a child ignoring a plain alphabet chart on the wall.
Cut back: same child fully focused on the velcro book page, fingers tracing the letter.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
> "Why does a phone hold attention for 2 hours but a book gets 2 seconds?"

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"I kept asking myself this. And that question became the whole design brief for our alphabet section."*

*"A flat letter does nothing for a toddler's brain. It has no story. No texture. No reason to stay."*

---

### Level 4 — Progress Reward (18–40s)

**[VISUAL — this is the design walkthrough section, show each beat as it's described]**

**Beat 1 — The Letter Tile**
Show the large tactile letter "A" velcro tile being held up, then pressed back onto the page with a satisfying click-rip sound.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
> "Step 1: Touch + feel the letter"

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"Every letter is a large velcro tile — the child picks it up, feels the shape in their hand, places it back. That's muscle memory building right there."*

---

**Beat 2 — Two Words Per Letter**
Show the page layout — "A for Apple, A for Ant" — with each word sitting beside a vivid 3D illustration.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
> "Step 2: Two words. Not one."

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"We gave every letter TWO words — not one. Because one word is a fact. Two words start a conversation. 'Apple is red. But Ant is so small!' That's language development happening in real time."*

---

**Beat 3 — The 3D Illustration Logic**
Tilt the book slightly so the light catches the 3D depth of the Apple illustration — it visually pops.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
> "Step 3: 3D illustrations that pull a child in"

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"We chose 3D-style illustrations deliberately. A flat drawing is easy to ignore. A 3D image triggers the brain the same way a toy does — it looks like something you can reach out and touch."*

*"That's the attention hook built right into the design."*

---

### Level 5 — Identity Payoff (40–52s)

**[VISUAL]**
Show a child working independently on the alphabet page — matching velcro letters, pointing at illustrations, talking to themselves.
Parent in the background, calm, watching from a distance.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]**
> "30 minutes. No screen. No asking for help. Just learning."

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"When a child is this engaged — they're not bored, they're not restless, and they're not reaching for your phone. They're actually learning."*

*"That's the feeling we designed this for."*

---

### Level 6 — Action Release (52–60s)

**[VISUAL]**
Quick final shot: close the book, show the cover — All In One Busy Book by SanskaTots.
Pull back to show the full book with the child's hand resting on it.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT — animated pop-in]**
> "All In One Busy Book — available on Amazon India"

**[VOICEOVER]**
*"Link in bio. Amazon delivery in 1 to 2 days."*

## Production Notes

| Element | Instruction |
|---|---|
| Opening sound | ASMR velcro rip — record in a quiet room, clip mic preferred |
| Lighting | Natural window light or softbox — 3D illustrations need side lighting to show depth |
| Camera | Macro / close-up lens or phone portrait mode for letter tile shots |
| Pacing | Fast cuts (0.5–1s each) for Level 3 and 4 beats — slow reveal for Level 1 |
| Child footage | If using a child, no face required — hands and reactions only works well |
| B-roll needed | 1. Velcro rip close-up, 2. 3D page tilt shot, 3. Letter tile in hand, 4. Child matching activity, 5. Book cover final shot |
