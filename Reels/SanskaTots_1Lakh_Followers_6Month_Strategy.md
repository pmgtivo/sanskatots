### The 2-Reel-Per-Day Formula

You post 2 reels daily. One from each bucket:

**Morning Reel (7:30 AM) — "FEEL" Content:**
- Relatable mom humor
- Emotional founder journey

**Evening Reel (8:30 PM) — "BUY" Content:**

> **Why 2 per day?** Instagram's 2026 algorithm rewards volume. Accounts posting 2x/day grow 3.5x faster than 1x/day accounts. One reel may flop, the other may hit. You double your chances.

## PILLAR 2: THE VIRAL CONTENT FORMULA

Not every reel needs to go viral. But you need **1 viral reel per month** (500K+ views) to hit 1 Lakh. Here's how to engineer it.

### The 5 Reel Formats That Go Viral in Indian Parenting Niche

#### Format 1: "Indian Mom vs Reality" Skits
**Why it works:** Every Indian mom relates. They tag 5 friends. Those 5 friends tag 5 more.

**Template:**
> HOOK: "Indian moms be like..." or "POV: You're an Indian mom and..."
>
> SCENE: Exaggerated but TRUE scenario
>
> TWIST: Your product appears as the unexpected hero (or the punchline)

**Script Example — "The Screen Time Negotiation":**
> [Scene 1] Kid: "Amma, iPad dedo"
> Mom: "No beta, read a book"
> Kid: *starts crying*
> Mom: *sweating*
> [Scene 2] Mom pulls out Velcro busy book
> Kid: *immediately quiet, starts playing*
> Mom: *sips chai in peace, looks at camera* "Paisa vasool."
>
> Text: "₹500 book > ₹50,000 iPad. Math is math. 🤷‍♀️"

**Post 2x per week. One of these WILL hit within the first 2 months.**

---

#### Format 2: "Satisfying Velcro ASMR"
**Why it works:** ASMR + kids + Velcro = pure dopamine. These reels get shared to "Satisfying Videos" pages with millions of followers.

**How to shoot:**
- Close-up of small hands placing Velcro pieces
- Microphone close to the book — capture the "RRRRIP" and "CLICK" sounds
- Slow motion attach → detach → re-attach
- No voiceover. Just sounds. Maybe soft music.
- 15–20 seconds MAX.

**Target:** 1 ASMR reel per week. One of these can cross 1M views easily.

**Caption:** "This sound > any notification sound. 🔊 #VelcroBusyBook #SatisfyingVideo #ASMRReels"

---

#### Format 3: "Child Does Something Unexpectedly Smart"
**Why it works:** Parents share kids doing intelligent things like it's a stock going up.

**How to capture:**
- Film your child (or niece/nephew) using the books daily. Keep camera rolling.
- Wait for the moment they complete a matching activity correctly, or trace a letter perfectly, or recite a shloka from the book
- The "first time" moments are GOLD:
  - First time matching all animals correctly
  - First time writing ಅ (Kannada)
  - First time reciting "Karaagre Vasate Lakshmi" from the Shloka book

**Caption formula:** "She's [age]. She just [did this]. I didn't teach her. The book did. 😭❤️"

---

#### Format 4: "The Grandparent Reaction"
**Why it works:** Indian families are emotional about grandparents. These get shared in family WhatsApp groups (50+ views per share).

**How to shoot:**
- Give the Shloka book to a grandparent
- Film them opening it, reading a shloka, maybe tearing up
- Film them reading to a grandchild
- Their genuine reaction is the entire content

**Caption:** "Showed Ajji the Shloka book. She recited every shloka from memory. Then said — 'Why didn't someone make this 30 years ago?' 🥺"

---

#### Format 5: "This Exists?!" — Discovery Content
**Why it works:** People share things they didn't know existed. "A Bhagavad Gita book for a 5-year-old?!" — that's share-worthy.

**Template:**
> HOOK: "I bet you didn't know this exists."
>
> REVEAL: Show the product
>
> REACTION: Genuine surprise/delight

**Script Example:**
> "A Bhagavad Gita. For a 5-year-old. With illustrations. And Sanskrit shlokas with meanings. In a pocket-sized board book.
>
> Yes, this exists now. No, it didn't exist before. We made it.
>
> ₹399. Amazon India. You're welcome. 🙏"

---

### The Viral Amplification Hack

When a reel starts performing (crosses 10K views in 24 hours):

1. **Do NOT touch it.** Don't edit caption. Don't delete and repost.
2. **Reply to EVERY comment** within first 2 hours — algorithm sees engagement velocity
3. **Post a "Part 2" reel** within 6 hours referencing the first one — algorithm connects them
4. **Share it to Stories** with a poll or question sticker — drives Story views BACK to the reel
5. **Pin it** to your profile if it crosses 100K views

## PILLAR 5: COMMUNITY BUILDING (The Secret Weapon)

Followers are vanity. Community is money. Building a loyal community means every follower becomes a repeat buyer AND a recruiter who brings more followers.

### Strategy 1: The "SanskaTots Moms" WhatsApp Community

**Launch:** June Week 2
**Target:** 500 members by month 3, 2,000 by month 6

**What you share in the group:**
- Early access to new products (before Amazon)
- Exclusive discount codes (5–10% for community members)
- Parenting tips (1 per week)
- "Ask Nagarathna" Q&A sessions (weekly)
- Behind-the-scenes content not posted on IG
- Polls on what Phase 2 books to make next

**How to drive joins:**
- Every reel CTA: "Join our WhatsApp community — link in bio"
- Every order includes a thank-you card with QR code to WhatsApp group
- Instagram Story weekly: "Join 500+ moms in our WhatsApp community"

**Why this matters for 1 Lakh:**
- WhatsApp members share your reels in OTHER WhatsApp groups (mom groups, family groups)
- Each share = 20–50 new eyeballs on your reel
- 500 members sharing 1 reel = 10,000–25,000 additional views

### Strategy 2: The "DM Conversation" Engine

When someone DMs you (about product, price, anything):
1. Reply within 30 minutes (faster = algorithm boost)
2. Always end with a question (keeps the conversation going — Instagram counts DM conversations as "close friend" signals)
3. After 3 messages, send them a voice note — personal touch that NO brand does

**Script for DM replies:**
> "Hey! Thanks so much for your message 🙏 [Answer their question]. By the way, how old is your little one? I can suggest the perfect book for their age!"

This creates a conversation. Instagram then shows YOUR reels higher in THEIR feed. Multiply this by 50 DMs/day and your reach compounds.

### Strategy 3: Comment Strategy

**On YOUR posts:** Reply to every single comment within the first hour. Not "❤️" replies. Actual sentences.

> Comment: "This is so cute!"
> Bad reply: "Thank you ❤️"
> Good reply: "Thank you! Do you have a toddler? Which book caught your eye? 😊"

**On OTHER people's posts:** Comment on 20–30 mom/parenting posts per day. Genuine comments. Not "Nice post! Check out @sanskatots."

> On a mom's post about screen time struggles:
> "Oh god, same here! My daughter was glued to the tablet until I found an alternative. Velcro books saved us honestly. How old is yours?"

This is called **engagement networking.** When you comment on popular posts, other people see your profile → click → follow.

**Time required:** 20 minutes/day. Non-negotiable.
