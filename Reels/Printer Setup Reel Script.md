# Printer Setup Reel Script

---

## Script

**(0:00 - 0:04):**
Everything is ready for launching our activity books on Amazon. 
But wait... I forgot very basic thing in my todo list.

**(0:04 - 0:14):**
Designs done. Books getting printed. Packaging sorted. 
ohh my god.. I thought I figured it out Everything in my todo list.
And then lately realised. we totally forgot printer for printing shipping labels.

**(0:14 - 0:24):**
So I Googled it, thinking it's a five-minute decision to purchase a printer.
But turns out there are multiple types of printers — InkJet, Direct Thermal, and more. that's when my head started spinning.


**(0:24 - 0:33):**
After spending hours researching, I choosed to get Direct Thermal printer.
Reason: No ink cartridges, prints fast, built exactly for shipping labels.

**(0:33 - 0:45):**
once I got the Printer, started setting up the label printer.
I spent more than hour just trying to get one label to print correctly and align perfectly on the sticker sheet.

**(0:45 - 0:55):**
But finally — a clean, perfectly aligned shipping label came out.
One more thing done before the June launch. 
stay tuned for latest updates.

## Visuals

1. **(0:00 - 0:04)** — Confident expression, maybe arms crossed or leaning back. Suddenly a subtle "uh oh" look creeps in. Direct camera address.

2. **(0:04 - 0:14)** — Quick B-roll cuts: book designs on screen, printed book samples, packaged boxes. Then freeze — cut back to Nagarathna with a realization expression, maybe snapping fingers or palm to forehead.

3. **(0:14 - 0:24)** — Screen recording or phone showing Google search results for "best printer for ecommerce shipping labels." Overwhelmed expression. Maybe swipe through a few results fast.

4. **(0:24 - 0:33)** — Show the Direct Thermal printer being unboxed or arriving. Quick confident nod — decision made.

5. **(0:33 - 0:45)** — Close-up of printer connected to laptop. Show the label settings screen — wrong size, misaligned print. Frustrated but calm expression. Clock in background or "1 hour later" text overlay.

6. **(0:45 - 0:55)** — Clean label printing out smoothly. Pick it up, inspect it, nod in approval. Phone or notebook showing to-do list — hand draws a checkmark. Small satisfied smile.

## Notes
- Keep energy grounded, not over-excited — the exhaustion mixed with quiet satisfaction is the tone.
- The "I forgot a basic ecommerce thing" moment is relatable to any small business owner — lean into the self-awareness, not embarrassment.
- End on a forward-looking note — "slowly, we are getting there" keeps viewers coming back for more.
