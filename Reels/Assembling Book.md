### Assembling Book

Do you know what goes into assembling a book? Well, let me take you behind the scenes of our busy day as we prepare to launch our activity books in June!
Today, workers came to work early in the morning to start assembling the books. as they were busy putting the pieces together, I was busy designing the product images for our Amazon listing.
I also spent some time validating the books and making sure the quality is up to our standards.
Assembling a book is not an easy task. It requires precision, care, and a lot of patience. To assemble just one book, we need to put over a hundreds of Velcro stickers in the right place. Our team is working hard to get these books out soon as possible, and I am happy seeing the progress we have made.
Later in the afternoon, I glued to my screen, designing our product images for the Amazon listing. We want these pictures to capture exactly how engaging and educational our books are.
June is right around the corner, and the pressure is officially on! Before I sign off to put the baby down, I have a tiny secret. I’ve also been quietly building our brand-new website... and honestly? It looks incredible. Make sure you follow, because in my next video, we’ll be launching the site! See you tomorrow!
