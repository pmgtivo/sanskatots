# 🎬 REEL SCRIPT – PEN SEARCH JOURNEY (Part 2 Continuation)

**Duration:** 45–60 seconds
**Mood:** Determined → Honest struggle → Community call-to-action
**BGM:** Light upbeat/curious background music — something like a "detective on a mission" or "behind the scenes" vibe
**Series Label:** Add text overlay on first frame — *"Part 2 of my pen journey 👀"*

---

## SHOT 1 (0:00–0:05) — HOOK / CALLBACK

**Visual:** Quick 1-second flashback clip or still from your previous reel (the pen-throwing moment). Then CUT to your face — looking determined, maybe arms crossed or hands on hips.
**Text overlay:** *"UPDATE: The pen search is ON 🔍"*
**Dialogue (direct to camera, confident tone):**
> "So I told you I lost money on those cheap pens. Now watch what happens next."

---

## SHOT 2 (0:05–0:15) — THE FAT PEN REJECTION

**Visual:** You hold up the fat/thick pen clearly showing its barrel size next to your hand. Then pick up a small child's hand (stuffed toy hand, or demo with your own fingers pinched close to show "small kid grip"). Show the mismatch. Shake your head slowly.

**Text overlay:** *"Good quality ✅ But… kids can't hold this 😬"*

**Dialogue:**
> "I found pens with actually good quality — they write well, ink is smooth, no issues. But look at the size. These are FAT. My activity books are for tiny little hands — 2 to 5-year-olds. This won't work. Rejected."

**Action beat:** You set the fat pens aside with a firm *thud* — same energy as throwing bad pens in Part 1.

---

## SHOT 3 (0:15–0:25) — THE ONGOING SEARCH

**Visual:** Show 2–3 different pen packets/samples laid out on a table. Maybe a notepad next to them with scribbles/test marks. You stand over them like you're evaluating.

**Text overlay:** *"Samples ordered from multiple vendors… waiting 📦"*

**Dialogue:**
> "So I've reached out to different vendors. Samples are on their way. Could take a few days to reach me here in Bangalore. Till then — the search continues."

**Optional insert shot:** Quick clip of you checking your phone/WhatsApp as if tracking a delivery. Makes it real and relatable.

---

## SHOT 4 (0:25–0:38) — WHAT I'M LOOKING FOR

**Visual:** Open your activity book and point to a page. Show the writing area. Then hold up fingers to indicate small size needed.

**Text overlay:** *"Slim grip. Good ink. Safe for kids. NON-NEGOTIABLE."*

**Dialogue:**
> "Here's what I need — pen slim enough for small hands to hold comfortably, ink that doesn't bleed through the page, colors that actually show up bright. Simple requirements. But finding the right one? Not so simple."

---

## SHOT 5 (0:38–0:52) — COMMUNITY CTA (Most Important Shot)

**Visual:** Look directly into camera. Warm but serious expression. Hold up the activity book facing the camera.

**Text overlay:** *"Do YOU know the perfect pen? Tell me 👇"*

**Dialogue:**
> "And this is where I need YOUR help. If you know any pen brand or specific pen that works well for kids activity books — please drop the name or a link in the comments. You might literally help hundreds of kids get a better experience. Let's find the perfect pen together."

---

## SHOT 6 (0:52–0:60) — OUTRO / BRAND CLOSE

**Visual:** SanskaTots logo or activity book with your handle. Smile at camera.
**Text overlay:** *"Follow for the update — when I find THE ONE ✅"*
**Dialogue (optional, or just text):**
> "Stay tuned. Part 3 is coming — and it will be the win."

## 📌 NOTES FOR SHOOTING

- Shoot in natural daylight near a window for crisp pen + product closeups
- The "fat pen vs small hand" comparison shot is your MOST IMPORTANT visual — spend extra time getting this right
- Keep the table clean/neutral background — white or wooden — so pens are the hero
- Film the "samples on table" shot in a flat lay style (camera pointing straight down) for a professional look
- Total shoot time: ~20 minutes max
