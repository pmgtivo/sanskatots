# Instagram Reel Script (≈60 Seconds)

**[0–3 sec | Hook]**
**Voiceover:**
*"What if one book could teach almost everything your child needs before school?"*

**[3–10 sec | Show the busy book opening]**
**Voiceover:**
*"When we designed the SanskaTots All-in-One Busy Book, we didn't just create activities... we carefully thought about how young children actually learn."*

**[10–22 sec | Show alphabet pages with Velcro letters]**
**Voiceover:**
*"Every alphabet has its own reusable Velcro letter. Children can pick it up, match it, touch it, and learn by doing. That's because little hands learn best when they're actively involved."*

**[22–34 sec | Show vocabulary words]**
**Voiceover:**
*"And every letter comes with two simple vocabulary words. This helps children build their vocabulary naturally. The more words they know, the easier it becomes to understand, speak, and later form simple English sentences with confidence."*

**[34–47 sec | Show number pages]**
**Voiceover:**
*"The number section is designed step by step. Kids learn numbers 1 to 10, identify each number, trace the numeral, trace the number word, count objects, and use reusable Velcro pieces to match every number. Learning becomes fun instead of memorizing."*

**[47–55 sec | Flip through remaining concepts]**
**Voiceover:**
*"And that's not all. The book also covers the essential preschool concepts children should know before joining school — all in one reusable, screen-free learning book."*

**[55–60 sec | Emotional ending + product shot]**
**Voiceover:**
*"We spent countless hours thinking through every page, every activity, and every little detail — so parents like you can spend less time preparing lessons and more time enjoying precious learning moments with your child. ❤️"*

**On-screen text:**
**"Designed with love. Made for curious little learners. 🌱"**
