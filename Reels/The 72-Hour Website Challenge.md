# REEL SCRIPT — "The 72-Hour Website Challenge (Part 1)"

**Series:** Website Journey (1 of 3)
**Duration:** 45–55 seconds
**Vibe:** Determined, slightly rebellious, and authentic. You are letting the audience in on a behind-the-scenes founder struggle.

---

## THE SCRIPT

### [0:00 – 0:04] — THE STORY HOOK *(The Contrarian Statement)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Start mid-action. You are sitting at your desk with a giant cup of coffee. The screen behind you shows a completely blank webpage or an empty code editor. You look directly into the camera with a determined, slightly crazy smile.

**Nagarathna says (fast, confident, hooking the viewer):**
> DO I need a website to sell my books online?
> *Ohh, shit..  yeahhhhhh......*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> DO I Need to build a Website??

---

### [0:04 – 0:18] — THE CONTEXT *(The Problem & The Easy Way Out)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Cut to a quick, proud shot of you arranging the SanskaTots activity books on a table. Then cut to you holding your phone, walking and talking naturally like you are explaining this to a friend.

**Nagarathna says (conversational, pacing):**

> *"Here is the situation. We are launching SanskaTots in just a few weeks. The books are ready, but we desperately need our own website.
> yes, we do need it for showcasing our books in best possible way for our clients.
> I got no investment left to spend on consultancy to build it for me, so no choice I should do it myself.
> When I did my research, literally every video and blog said to use shopify platform which is easiest. And the plugins do everything for you. You can set it up in a day.'"*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

> *The advice: Shopify is king 👑 (Easy, fast, built-in plugins)*

---

### [0:18 – 0:30] — THE CONFLICT *(The "Desi Founder" Reality)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Quick zoom-in on your face. You give a very practical, "let's be real" expression. Maybe pop up a screenshot of Shopify's monthly USD pricing next to your head.

**Nagarathna says (practical, a bit frustrated):**

> *"And they weren't wrong. It IS easy. But nobody talks about the monthly subscription fees. As a bootstrapped founder, paying thousands of rupees every single month before I even sales. That math just doesn't work for me right now."*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

> *The catch: Monthly subscriptions in $$$ 📉*
> *Bootstrapped founder math = Keep overheads LOW.*

---

### [0:30 – 0:45] — THE TWIST & THE CHALLENGE *(The Hard Path)*

**VISUAL DIRECTION:**

> Fast montage: You tying your hair up in a bun, opening up an AWS console or a code editor, and typing fast. Energy shifts to "builder mode."

**Nagarathna says (energized, confident):**

> So, I am taking the difficult path. I am putting my engineer hat back on and building a custom website completely from scratch to keep our expenses low.
> Oh, and I am giving myself exactly 72 hours to do it.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

> *The Plan: Custom build from scratch. 👩‍💻*
> *Deadline: 72 Hours ⏱️*
